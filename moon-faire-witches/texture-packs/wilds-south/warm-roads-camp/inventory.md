# The Warm Roads camp (`warm-roads-camp`): texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/wilds/walk-warm-roads-camp.png` (1536 x 1024). Night.

The camp on the Warm Roads where the airship lands, on a heather moor at night. A roped landing ground of worn grass on the west; in the middle, a round stone hut with a turf roof, two canvas tents, chests and stumps round a cooking fire; the old Warm Road (flagstones with glowing amber seams) running east; dirt paths north to a lookout and south over a stream on a slab bridge. Everything else is heather, gorse, boulders, birches and low dry-stone walls.

Where a row says "made in" another place, its texture, prompt and reference crop are in that place's folder; here it is only listed, with where it shows in this painting. "In this group" means it is used across these four wild places (see the group README).
