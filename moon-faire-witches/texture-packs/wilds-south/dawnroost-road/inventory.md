# The forest road to Dawnroost (`dawnroost-road`): texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/wilds/walk-dawnroost-forest-road.png` (1536 x 1024). Night.

The old Warm Road running north through a dark forest to Dawnroost, at night. Its mossy flagstones pass an iron lantern on a post and a milestone carved with a sun. Earth trails lead west to a still pool ringed with flat stones under a great oak, and east to a woodcutter's clearing with a lean-to, firewood, a chopping block and an axe. Round them: big oaks, young firs, a few birches, boulders, ferns, flowering undergrowth and a fallen mossy log.

Where a row says "made in" another place, its texture, prompt and reference crop are in that place's folder; here it is only listed, with where it shows in this painting. "In this group" means it is used across these four wild places (see the group README).
