# Eldergrove's edge (`eldergrove-edge`): texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/wilds/walk-eldergrove-edge.png` (1536 x 1024). Night.

The edge of the ancient Eldergrove forest at night, with a light frost. A wide pebbly earth track crosses the middle. At the top, a giant elder tree with a dark hollow between its roots that a cobbled path leads into; on the top right, a ring of standing stones (one carved with a moon, one with a sun); bottom left, a ruined stone arch with a carved pillar. Round them: huge mossy roots, young firs, bare twisted trees, ferns, red and frosted undergrowth, glowing pale mushrooms, fallen logs and boulders.

Where a row says "made in" another place, its texture, prompt and reference crop are in that place's folder; here it is only listed, with where it shows in this painting. "In this group" means it is used across these four wild places (see the group README).
