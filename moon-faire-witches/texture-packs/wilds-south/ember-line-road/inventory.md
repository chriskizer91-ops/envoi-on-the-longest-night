# The Ember Line road (`ember-line-road`): texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/wilds/walk-ember-line-road.png` (1536 x 1024). Night.

The old Warm Road crossing the heather moor along the Ember Line, at night. The road (flagstones with amber light in their seams) comes in from the west, crosses a stream on a humped stone bridge and bends north. Three node stones (standing stones with a round glassy eye) stand in a ring of standing stones on the west, beside the road by the bridge, and on a rise in the east. Round them: heather, gorse, boulders, birches, dry-stone walls.

Where a row says "made in" another place, its texture, prompt and reference crop are in that place's folder; here it is only listed, with where it shows in this painting. "In this group" means it is used across these four wild places (see the group README).
