# The northern camp: what a 3D build needs

A camp in a frosted pine wood in the north, where the airship lands. Seen from above at a slant, at night: cold blue
moonlight everywhere, with warm firelight and lantern light round the camp. On the left, a big square landing ground of
frosted grass fenced by four wooden stakes and a sagging rope. In the middle, two canvas tents, a stone fire ring with a
fire, log stumps for seats, firewood, barrels and a crate, and a lantern hanging from a post. A packed-earth road runs
east out of the picture and branches north to a lit hollow under a huge mossy boulder, and south down to a little plank
bridge over a dark, half-frozen stream. Everything else is dense frosted firs, bare white birches, mossy boulders and low
frosted undergrowth with amber flowers and purple heather. No buildings.
