# The Thornwood: texture pack inventory

The wild wood east of Wickhollow at night. The earth road comes in from the west over an old stone bridge (one lantern on its parapet) across a stream with small falls and lily pads, runs past a clearing where the moon stone stands (a tall standing stone with a crescent cut in it, among mossy rocks and white flowers), and goes on east to Bogmire. Everything off the road is dead, twisted thorn trees and thick bramble with dark red berries, mossy grey boulders and broken stumps, with low banks of cold blue mist lying between the trees. Lit only by the moon and the one lantern. The fight painting of the same bridge adds the sky, and thorny vines with red roses on the parapets.

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-thornwood.png` (1536 x 1024). The walking map is seen from above at a slant, so ground cut from it looks squashed top to bottom; the stand-ins of ground were stretched back (1.3 to 1.4 times, 2.6 for the fight painting's meadow).
