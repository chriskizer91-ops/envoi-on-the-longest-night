# Io's cottage: texture pack inventory

Io's home in the woods south of the square, at night: a small stone cottage with a thick mossy roof, a round lit window and a smoking chimney, a woodshed lean-to and a herb drying rack; in front, her walled and fenced garden of lavender, white globe flowers, greens and blue delphiniums, a potting bench, a rain barrel, a rose bower, a cauldron on a tripod by a stump, and a stone garden gate with two lanterns. Worn paths of cobbles in pinkish earth wind round it: north up the lane to the square, west over a plank footbridge across a stream with small falls and a lily pond, south to the road, east past a huge ancient oak hung with moss to a lantern post where the forest path starts. Dense blue-green woods with mossy boulders fill the rest. The tops of two of the square's houses show along the top edge.

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-wickhollow-cottage.png` (1536 x 1024). The walking map is seen from above at a slant, so ground cut from it looks squashed top to bottom; the stand-ins of ground were stretched back (1.3 to 1.4 times, 2.6 for the fight painting's meadow).
