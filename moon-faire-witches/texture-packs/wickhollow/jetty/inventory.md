# The jetty: texture pack inventory

The lake west of the square at night, where Quill moors his skiff. A cobbled lane comes down from the square between two lamp pillars to a little cobbled quay with low walls of rounded stones and four more lamps; from the quay, stone steps lead down to a long plank pier with four lamp posts, barrels, coils of rope and thick mooring posts, and a narrow plank arm makes a mooring square to its east. Quill's stall stands east of the lane under a red-and-cream striped awning, with crates, barrels and net racks. Weeping willows, reeds, lily pads and boulders line the shore; leafy woods behind. The black lake fills the bottom half, with the moon's glitter on the left.

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-wickhollow-jetty.png` (1536 x 1024). The walking map is seen from above at a slant, so ground cut from it looks squashed top to bottom; the stand-ins of ground were stretched back (1.3 to 1.4 times, 2.6 for the fight painting's meadow).
