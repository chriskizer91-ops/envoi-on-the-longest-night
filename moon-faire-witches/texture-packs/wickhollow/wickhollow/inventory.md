# Wickhollow (the town square): texture pack inventory

Io's home town at night: a round cobbled square with the Moonwell in the middle (Lunara's resting place), ringed by a low flower-bed wall, two benches and four iron street lamps. North: the little stone chapel behind an iron railing, its churchyard of old headstones. North-west: Nettie's big timber-framed house and her striped market stall. North-east: Hilde's forge with its glowing hearth. East: an old stone bridge with four lamp pillars over a river with small falls (the way to the Thornwood). Houses with red tile roofs, gardens, stone walls and picket fences all round; a dark lake with lily pads and two small wooden jetties on the west (the lower one is the way to the jetty); the south road leads down to Io's cottage. Thick dark woods fill the corners. Warm orange lamplight pools on the cobbles; everything else is cool moonlit blue.

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-wickhollow-square.png` (1536 x 1024). The walking map is seen from above at a slant, so ground cut from it looks squashed top to bottom; the stand-ins of ground were stretched back (1.3 to 1.4 times, 2.6 for the fight painting's meadow).
