Envoi in 3D: pictures to make

/*N*/ prompts for the textures that will turn Envoi's places into 3D: every walking map, every fight place, and the world seen from the Magpie. Moon Faire's prompt rules, used as they are, so the new pictures match the way Moon Faire's were made.

Texture packs, not building photos

The 3D paints flat pictures onto simple shapes. So the pictures to make are of two kinds:

  • Textures (ground and material): seamless squares of cobbles, grass, snow, planks, stone, bark and water. One square covers every patch of that ground in every place, at any size.
  • Flat views (flat views, roofs and plants): one picture each of the few things a texture cannot describe, such as a stall front, a door, a sign or a tree.

Until a picture comes back, the 3D uses a rough stand-in cut straight from your painting. Each card shows its stand-in.

How to make them

  1. For each prompt, attach the pictures shown on its card: the close-up, and the whole painting. They are all in the zip that came with this page, under the names on the card.
  2. Paste the prompt. Each one is complete, with its rules included.
  3. Save the result under the file name on the card.
  4. Zip a round together and send it back.

If a picture comes back with a lamp glow, a shadow, or perspective in it, ask for it again. Those three are what spoil a texture.
