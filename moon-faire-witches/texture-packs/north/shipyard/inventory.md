# The shipyard: texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-shipyard.png` (1536 x 1024). Boxes are x0, y0, x1, y1 in its pixels.

Ysmera Brightkeel's walled yard on a rock in a dark, ice-strewn sea, reached by a long stone bridge from the south. Crenellated grey stone walls with fire baskets burning on the corner towers ring a yard of warm-lit cobbles. In the middle is the slipway with its big timber cradle, where the Magpie is built, with two wooden cranes. West: Old Gil's bunkhouse and a tall timber-framed house; east: the forge, a log shed and Pim's hall with dormer windows. A twin-towered gatehouse with navy-and-gold sun banners faces the bridge. Stairs lead down to a west pier and an east quay with a crane. Roofs are slate with snow on them; the cliffs below are snowy with small pines. Lamp posts and windows give warm light everywhere in the yard.

Rows marked shared use one texture for the whole game. "made here" means its stand-in and prompt are in this folder; "listed" means it is made in another place (see the group README).
