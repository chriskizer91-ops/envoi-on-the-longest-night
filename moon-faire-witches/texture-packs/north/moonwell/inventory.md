# The dead Moonwell: texture pack inventory

Painting: `reference/art/walk/walk-misthollow-moonwell.png` (1536 x 1024). Boxes are x0, y0, x1, y1 in its pixels.

The round court above Misthollow where the final battle happens. A wide court of frosted setts laid in sweeping arcs surrounds the dead Moonwell on its ringed dais: a big round well whose mouth is black and empty, with a carved band of crescent moons and a moth round its side and a twisted iron frame over it. The north keep's arched door, two round crenellated towers, and pale grey stone buildings with snowy slate roofs ring the court; navy banners with pale crescent moons hang on the towers. The only way in is up the steps from the south street, between two iron fire bowls (the only warm light) and two iron gateposts. Small walled gardens with frosted bare shrubs and thin snow fill the corners. Everything is cold and blue; no window is lit.
