Wickhollow square (Pilot)

Highly detailed hand-painted fantasy RPG ground-level map in a rich painterly pixel-art style, for a game where a small pixel character walks on top of it. A high overhead view, straight on and not rotated: you see the ground from above and the front faces of walls, houses and trees, like a classic 16-bit JRPG town map but richly painted. Never isometric, no horizon, no sky. Night: deep violet and indigo shadows, silver-blue moonlight from the upper left with soft short shadows to the lower right, warm amber light pooling around lamps and lit windows. Roads, paths, bridges, doorways and open yards are clear and easy to read, never hidden under roofs or branches. A doorway is about one thirtieth of the picture's width. No people, animals, creatures, ships, text, lettering, UI, borders, grid lines or vignette. Landscape 3:2, at least 1536 × 1024.

Wickhollow's square, the heart of a small village where a dark forest meets a slow black river. A round cobbled square with an old stone well in the middle, inside a low ring wall, with an iron frame and a lantern over it; the water in the well gives off a faint silver glow. At the top, a small stone chapel with a round rose window, and a graveyard behind iron railings. Round the square: crooked half-timbered houses with steep dark-red roofs and warm lit windows, a blacksmith's forge glowing under an open shed, and a market stall with a striped awning. Iron lamp posts round the square. Roads leave the picture at the right edge toward an old stone bridge, at the bottom edge down a lane to a cottage, and at the left edge down to the river jetty.

Single map at 1536x1024. Camera requirement is critical: orthographic overhead JRPG tilemap, all north-south walls and paths vertical and east-west walls and paths horizontal, no perspective convergence. Buildings small enough for doors approximately 51 pixels wide. Fill the entire frame with ground and architecture: absolutely no visible moon, sky, horizon, boat or ship. Broad connected open pathways with easy access to chapel and all house doors. Keep roofs and branches away from paths.
