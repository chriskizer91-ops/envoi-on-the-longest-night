Walking maps — selected production prompts

All 13 locations from 04-walking-maps.md are included, including the two optional maps. Built-in image generation used. Two candidates generated per map and selected visually for route clarity. Existing pilots retained. Wickhollow square pilot supplied as style reference for the ten remaining maps; named battle-reference paintings were unavailable. Images are visual map assets; movement, collision boundaries and character scale require in-game testing.

Io's cottage
Filename: walk-wickhollow-cottage.png
Selected candidate: 1

Highly detailed hand-painted fantasy RPG ground-level map in a rich painterly pixel-art style, for a game where a small pixel character walks on top of it. A high overhead view, straight on and not rotated: you see the ground from above and the front faces of walls, houses and trees, like a classic 16-bit JRPG town map but richly painted. Never isometric, no horizon, no sky. Night: deep violet and indigo shadows, silver-blue moonlight from the upper left with soft short shadows to the lower right, warm amber light pooling around lamps and lit windows. Roads, paths, bridges, doorways and open yards are clear and easy to read, never hidden under roofs or branches. A doorway is about one thirtieth of the picture's width. No people, animals, creatures, ships, text, lettering, UI, borders, grid lines or vignette. Landscape 3:2, at least 1536 × 1024.

A witch's cottage at the edge of the village: a small crooked cottage with a mossy roof, a round window and a lit lantern by the door. A fenced herb garden in neat beds of lavender, pale moon-white flowers and bell-shaped blooms, with a potting bench, a rain barrel, drying racks and a garden gate. A lane leaves at the top edge toward the village square. A forest path leaves at the right edge into a dark wood of huge twisted oaks with hanging moss.

Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match painterly pixel detail, colors and materials, not its village layout. Create the requested different location. Critical camera: orthographic high overhead JRPG map with axis-aligned architecture, no perspective convergence, no isometric rotation. No visible moon, sky, horizon, lettering, people, creatures, boats or airships. Empty landing and mooring spaces. Maintain continuous clearly visible walkable routes to specified exits and doorways. Doorways approximately 51 pixels wide; a future character will stand about 73 pixels tall. Output one complete 1536x1024 landscape map.
