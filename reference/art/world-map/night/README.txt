AETHERMOOR — NATURAL NIGHT

Nine night variants of the supplied daytime regions. The arrangement and file stems match the supplied pack: 01 02 03 across the top, 04 05 06 in the middle, 07 08 09 across the bottom. Each tile is 1536 x 1024. The full atlas is 4608 x 3072. placement.json gives exact positions.

Night treatment: cool moonlight, dark natural water/foliage/rock, and modest lights at existing buildings. No glowing routes or magical terrain lines. These are image-generation relighting edits of the daylight originals, with minor painted variations possible. The atlas is the direct assembly of the nine delivered tiles.

Extract both ZIPs into the same folder. Part 1 contains tiles 01–04, full atlas WebP, overview, coordinates and prompts. Part 2 contains tiles 05–09 and this readme. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Original daytime files remain separate.
