AETHERMOOR: BATTLE BACKGROUNDS 11–14

Four selected original PNGs for Moonlight in the Aether, continuing the approved combat-background pack.
All images are 1448 × 1086 pixels (4:3), with no resizing, recompression, or artwork edits after generation.
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and the same camera guide, approved Colossus painting, and Magpie world map references.
Two candidates were generated for each scene; the Moonwell received a third layout refinement.

Exact game-ready filenames and fight mapping:
11-verdant-wilds-warm-roads-moor.png — Band 2 wilds, levels 6–10.
12-thornhollow-dawnroost-living-node.png — Gate 10, three wraiths at Dawnroost's living node.
13-northern-crossroads.png — Gate 15, Halcyon's ambush.
14-ironhold-misthollow-dead-moonwell.png — Level 20 finale against Noctara and Halcyon.

Misthollow in the finale is the mountain town marked Ironhold on the map, distinct from the unused drowned fen ruins in image 04.
