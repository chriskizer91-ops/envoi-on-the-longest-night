## How to make them

1. **Start with the two hidden ones** (1 and 7). Keep the best of each, then attach your favourite to every other prompt as the reference for the style, so all twenty match.
2. **Square, 1:1, at least 1024 × 1024.** The game shrinks them to fit a phone.
3. **The object alone, in the middle,** with a clear shape that still reads very small: on the phone each picture shows at about the size of a thumbnail.
4. **A plain deep indigo background** (#151236, the colour of the game's menus), so the game can frame it. Nothing else in the picture: no hands, no table, no text.
5. Generate each two to four times and keep the one that is clearest when you look at it small.
6. Save them in `reference/art/items/` under the file names below.

What happens next: Claude shrinks each picture to 256 × 256 (about 10 to 20 KB each, so about 300 KB for all twenty, well inside the game's 30 MB) and shows it on the Items page, and in the moment each one is found.

## Rules for all of them

- **Things people made or found, worn and loved.** Old metal rubbed bright where hands held it, small dents, worn cloth. Nothing new and shiny.
- **The two hidden ones are the rarest in the world.** Only they have the faint white starlight shimmer round them, so they read as special next to the other eighteen.
- **Colours** stay out of the other lanes, as for the creatures: no deep purple (Noctara's), no soul-green (the wraiths'). Frost's pale blue-white is fine. Warm glowing accents read best on the dark menus.

## Style lock

Every prompt below starts with this.

```text
A single fantasy keepsake painted as an item picture for a JRPG's inventory, in the spirit of Final Fantasy IX's art: richly hand-painted with crisp clean edges and fine detail in its materials (worn metal, glass, cloth, wood, stone), not photoreal, not pixel art, not anime cel shading. The one object alone, in the middle, filling about two thirds of the frame, in a gentle three-quarter view from slightly above, with a strong clear silhouette that still reads when shrunk very small. Night lighting: soft cool moonlight from the upper left and a warm amber glow from below right. A plain deep indigo background (#151236) with a soft dark vignette and nothing else on it. No hands, no people, no table, no frame, no text, no letters. Square, 1:1, at least 1024 by 1024.
```

Each prompt below is the style lock followed by its own paragraph.
