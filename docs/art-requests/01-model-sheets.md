## 3. Wisp

A wisp is a soul starting to go hollow; a wraith is what it becomes (lore answer 16). It needs to read as a smaller, earlier cousin of the soul-green wraith, and come in groups. Move names are a first draft; their numbers come with the battle steps.

### Model sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, made as reference for a 3D modeler. Clean readable shapes, strong silhouette, soft painterly textures with crisp edges. Storybook JRPG fantasy: not photoreal, not pixel art. Night-world palette: deep blue shadows, glowing accents. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery. Same scale, same design and same colors in every view.

Creature model sheet of a wisp: a lost soul starting to go hollow, about 0.8 m tall, floating a little above the ground. A teardrop-shaped flame of pale, sickly soul-green light, brightest at the top, thinning into a long trailing tail of smoke. A small sorrowful face in the upper flame: two dark hollow eyes and a small open mouth. At its centre, a dark hollow is starting to open where its light is going out, ringed with a faint black edge. Two thin wavering tendrils of light like arms. A few small ember-specks orbit it. Palette: pale soul-green, white core, smoky grey-green tail, black hollow.

Top row: four views side by side, front, left side, back and three-quarter front. Orthographic with no perspective, all four the same height, with a thin height ruler in meters at the left. Bottom row: three faces (calm, hungry, wailing), the hollow at its centre close up, and the frost variant from the front: the same wisp in icy pale blue and white with small frost crystals in its tail. Small labels only.
```

### Action sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, storybook JRPG fantasy. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery.

Action pose sheet of a wisp, a 0.8 m floating teardrop flame of pale soul-green light with a small sorrowful face, a dark hollow opening at its centre, thin tendril arms and a trailing smoky tail. Eight poses in a 4 x 2 grid, same design and scale in every pose, a short label under each: 1 idle, bobbing and flickering; 2 Flicker, darting forward and flinging a spark of green soul-fire; 3 Cling, wrapping its tendrils around an unseen target and pulling light out of it; 4 Gutter, shrinking almost to nothing to dodge; 5 Wail, mouth wide with pale rings of sound; 6 hurt, torn and guttering; 7 the frost variant in icy blue and white, breathing out a plume of frost; 8 released, unravelling into soft light as a small pale moth rises out of it.
```
