# The 3D world: the short list of pictures that would make it better

From the reskin of all four zones (November 17, 2026). Chris asked for this in place of round three's 235 pictures.

**28 pictures, in five groups, most useful first.** Each prompt below is complete: paste it whole into the session that made rounds one and two, attach the pictures it names, and save the result under the file name on its card.

## How to use this

1. **Same session, same way.** These prompts use the same rule wording as rounds one and two, so the pictures come out in the same hand.
2. **Attach what each card names.** `made/...` is one of your own finished textures (in the zips you made, or `moon-faire-witches/texture-packs/made/` in the repository). `paintings/...` is the place's painting, as in the round one and two zips.
3. **Keep the file name on the card.** That is how the game finds the picture and how it stays labeled for your future games.
4. **Hand them back as zips**, as before. Nothing here changes the game until it is brought in.
5. **You don't have to make them all.** Groups A and B change the houses the most. Group D changes the trees the most, but needs a build step afterwards.

## What I saw, zone by zone

- **Zone 1, Wickhollow and the fen.** Your wall, roof and ground textures carry it. What stands out now: every timber-framed house has the same frame (group A), doors and windows are plain shapes (group B), barrels and posts look like floorboards (group C), and the trees and bushes are lumps (group D). Bogmire's dark heart: its deck goes nearly black (group E).
- **Zone 2, the Warm Roads.** Dawnroost is grey stone on grey stone: its houses, towers and great walls are all close in colour, so the town reads as one mass (the warmer second stone and the dressed stone in group A would set them apart). The moor and the roads look good. Bushes and firs as in zone 1.
- **Zone 3, the northern wilds.** The shipyard is mostly posts and beams, all in floor planks (the oak beam in group C would change it most). The northern camp's pines still use a stand-in bark (group C). Firs are smooth cones (group D).
- **Zone 4, the northeast peaks.** Misthollow's houses and its castle walls share one frosted stone (group A). The snow, ice and the frozen waterfall look good. Snowy firs are smooth cones (group D).

## What not to make

Round three as it was written (about 150 flat house fronts, 10 roofs from above, about 90 plants): the 3D world builds its houses from wall textures and its roofs from the tiled roof textures you already made, so those pictures would not be used.
