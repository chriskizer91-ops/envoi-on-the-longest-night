## 1. The Gloamwing

From the Lore & Party Bible's Hollow: "The Gloamwing grows fat on soul-light that should have gone up." It hunts the pale moths, the souls on their way to Auros. Moths mistake every lamp for the Moon, so it carries its own: a glowing sac like a small moon, where the souls it swallows flutter. Beaten, it lets them all go.

Its colors stay out of the other lanes: smoke-brown and ivory, with a moon-silver glow, not Noctara's purple or the wraith's green.

### Model sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, made as reference for a 3D modeler. Clean readable shapes, strong silhouette, soft painterly textures with crisp edges. Storybook JRPG fantasy: not photoreal, not pixel art. Night-world palette with warm glowing accents. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery. Same scale, same design and same colors in every view.

Creature model sheet of the Gloamwing, a great night-flier of the marshes that hunts the pale moths that are souls on their way to the Moon. About 3 m tall standing and 9 m from wingtip to wingtip. A heart-shaped face disc like a barn owl's, ivory with soft grey edges, with two huge round black eyes ringed in pale gold and a small hooked ivory beak; two short feathered tufts like ears. A deep chest and hunched shoulders covered in soft smoky grey-brown down like a moth's fur, darker on the back. Bat-like wings: long thin arm and finger bones stretching a thin smoke-brown membrane that glows warm amber where light shines through it, with fine darker veins and a ragged trailing edge, and a hooked claw at each wrist. Under its chest hangs a round glowing sac like a small full moon, pale silver-white and softly translucent, with the dark silhouettes of a few moths fluttering inside it. Strong grey legs with hooked black talons, and a short fan of long soft tail plumes. Palette: smoke-brown, slate grey, ivory, moon-silver glow, pale gold eye rings.

Top row: four full-body views side by side, front, left side, back and three-quarter front, standing upright on its talons with both wings spread wide. Orthographic with no perspective, all four the same height on one ground line, with a 1.7 m human silhouette beside it for scale. Bottom row: the face from the front and in profile, one wing folded, one wing open with light shining through it, the glowing moon-sac close up with moths inside, a foot with talons. Small labels only.
```

### Action sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, storybook JRPG fantasy. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery.

Action pose sheet of the Gloamwing, a night-flier 9 m from wingtip to wingtip with a pale barn-owl face, smoky grey-brown down, smoke-brown bat-like wings that glow amber where light shines through, and a glowing moon-white sac under its chest with moths fluttering inside. Eight full-body poses in a 4 x 2 grid, same design and scale in every pose, a short label under each: 1 hovering, wings mid-beat; 2 Moonlure, wings wrapped forward like a cloak around the brightly glowing sac, pale moths drifting toward it; 3 Swoop, diving low with talons thrust forward; 4 Wing Gale, reared up in the air with both wings in one huge downbeat, a blast of wind below; 5 Hush, wings folded tight around its body like a shroud, eyes half closed; 6 Glut, beak wide open, swallowing a glowing moth; 7 hurt, recoiling in the air with its wings thrown back; 8 released, fallen on the ground with its wings spread, the sac split open and a spiral of pale glowing moths rising out of it.
```

## 2. The Emberback

A giant salamander of the Ember Line. It lives in the veins of buried sunstone and basks on lit Warm Road nodes. Noctara's cold is dimming the nodes, so it is starving, and it comes up out of the earth hunting any warmth it can find. Beaten, it curls up asleep and cools to stone, and Sol can relight the node it was guarding.

### Model sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, made as reference for a 3D modeler. Clean readable shapes, strong silhouette, soft painterly textures with crisp edges. Storybook JRPG fantasy: not photoreal, not pixel art. Night-world palette with warm glowing accents. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery. Same scale, same design and same colors in every view.

Creature model sheet of the Emberback, a giant salamander that lives in veins of buried sunstone. About 7 m long from snout to tail tip and 1.6 m high at the shoulders, low and heavy, with four thick splayed legs and broad clawed feet. A broad, flat, rounded head with a wide mouth that glows orange inside and small bright amber eyes. Smooth black hide with a wet sheen, cracked all over like cooling lava; molten amber-gold light glows through every crack, brightest along the spine and fading toward the belly, which is dark grey-brown. A ridge of jagged sunstone crystals runs down its back from the neck to the tail, glowing gold from inside, the biggest over the shoulders. The long, thick tail tapers to a cluster of glowing crystals. Wisps of heat and a few embers rise off its back. Palette: basalt black, molten amber, gold, deep orange, a touch of ember red.

Top row: the whole creature in left side view, from above, from the front and in three-quarter front view, standing, with a 1.7 m human silhouette beside it for scale. Orthographic, no perspective. Bottom row: the head from the front and the side with the mouth open, the crystal ridge close up, a front foot, the crystals at the tail tip, and a patch of the cracked, glowing hide. Small labels only.
```

### Action sheet

```text
Stylized 3D creature art like a hand-painted collectible figurine, storybook JRPG fantasy. Even flat studio lighting, no cast shadows, plain light grey background (#D9D9D9), no scenery.

Action pose sheet of the Emberback, a 7 m giant salamander with black lava-cracked hide glowing molten gold through the cracks and a ridge of glowing sunstone crystals down its back. Eight full-body poses in a 4 x 2 grid, same design and scale in every pose, a short label under each: 1 idle, low to the ground, crystals softly pulsing; 2 bursting up out of a glowing crack in the ground; 3 Tail Lash, the tail whipping round in a blazing arc; 4 Ember Spit, head thrown forward spitting a glob of molten fire; 5 Heat Drain, mouth open, drawing ribbons of fire toward itself, its crystals blazing white-gold; 6 Eruption, reared up on its hind legs, front feet about to slam down; 7 starving, dim and dull red, frost creeping over its edges; 8 asleep, curled up like a cat, its glow gone out, cooled to dark stone.
```
