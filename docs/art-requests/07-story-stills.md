# Art Request 07: Story Stills

The game tells its big moments as painted stills with slow camera moves and the dialogue box over them, as FF9 did with its FMVs (plan, phase 5). Today the prologue drifts over the Night square painting and the other scenes play over the map Io stands on. These nine stills replace that.

How to make them:

- **Landscape 3:2, 1536 × 1024 or larger.** The game shows them full screen, drifting slowly, and crops the sides on a phone held upright, so keep what matters in the middle third.
- **Night,** in the battle backdrops' painted style (request 03): rich, detailed, moonlit and lamplit, not photoreal and not pixel art.
- **The characters exactly as their sheets show them.** Attach the sheets named for each still. Io, Sol, Halcyon, Noctara and Lunara are all women.
- Leave the bottom fifth calm and dark: the dialogue box sits there.
- Save them in `reference/art/stills/` under the names below.

## Style lock

Every prompt starts with this.

```text
A painted story still for a fantasy JRPG cutscene, in the spirit of Final Fantasy IX's pre-rendered scenes: richly detailed hand-painted night illustration, cinematic composition, soft moonlight and warm lamplight, deep blues and violets with amber highlights. Not photoreal, not pixel art, not anime cel shading. No text, no frame, no UI. Landscape 3:2, at least 1536 by 1024. Keep the bottom fifth calm and dark.
```

Each prompt below is the style lock followed by its own paragraph.

**1. The longest night is coming** (`still-prologue.png`). Attach the Night square backdrop.

```text
The village of Wickhollow at night, seen from above its square: the lamplit cottages, the stone well with its silver water, the little chapel. The sky above is wrong: a pale full moon in a sky with no stars at all, utterly black around it. A single small figure in a magenta witch's hat stands at a cottage door below, looking up.
```

**2. Sol arrives** (`still-sol.png`). Attach Sol's model sheet and Io's.

```text
An old stone bridge at night, its lamps just gone dark, mist on the river below. A young swordswoman in bronze half-plate and a midnight-blue cape runs across it toward the viewer, her sword blazing with amber sunfire, copper braid flying. At the bridge's near end the young witch Io, in her magenta hat and robe and round glasses, turns toward her, a violet glow in one hand.
```

**3. Quill's skiff** (`still-magpie.png`). Attach the Magpie's sheet (`art/airship/skiff-sheet.webp` in 20-min) and Io's and Sol's sheets.

```text
A lamplit wooden jetty on a dark lake at night. A small old wooden skiff with folded sail-wings and five dull crystals in brass cups along its deck is moored at the end. An old sailor in a navy coat and cap, a crow on his shoulder, hands a brass key to the young witch Io in her magenta hat; the swordswoman Sol stands behind her.
```

**4. Bogmire's lights come home** (`still-lights.png`). Attach the Bogmire walking map.

```text
A stilt town of wooden houses on a fen at night. Dozens of small golden lights fly over the black water like fireflies, each one streaming into a dark window, and the windows light up one by one. On the boardwalk the young witch Io and the swordswoman Sol watch; townsfolk lean out of doorways.
```

**5. Envoi is made** (`still-envoi.png`). Attach Envoi's model sheet, the Dawnroost node backdrop and Io's and Sol's sheets.

```text
A walled stone courtyard at night around a great glowing amber sunstone crystal. The young witch Io kneels, letting a bundle of old paper letters fly from her hands; they fold themselves in the air into a long paper wyrm with lantern-like scales. The swordswoman Sol touches her blazing sword to the wyrm's chest, and its heart catches warm fire.
```

**6. Spared** (`still-spared.png`). Attach Halcyon's, Sol's and Io's sheets and the northern crossroads backdrop.

```text
A ruined stone crossroads in a cold northern wilderness at night. The young witch Io lies fallen on the paving. The swordswoman Sol, hurt, stands over her with her sword raised. Facing her, a tall knight in dark teal armor with a cold blue glowing blade has stopped mid-stride, blade lowered, looking at Sol. Frost on the stones.
```

**7. The shipyard** (`still-shipyard.png`). Attach the shipmaster's portrait, the shipyard walking map and the Magpie's sheet.

```text
A snowy airship yard at night, lit by forges and lanterns. The little skiff stands in a wooden cradle on the slip while gnome shipwrights in tall star-blue caps rig her with a shimmering silver moon-sail. A tall elegant elf woman of the moon's people, the shipmaster, watches with the witch Io and the swordswoman Sol.
```

**8. The dead Moonwell** (`still-moonwell.png`). Attach the dead Moonwell backdrop and Noctara's and Halcyon's sheets.

```text
A courtyard of pale stone towers at night, snow falling, a great round well at its center with no light in it at all, like a hole in the world. Beside it waits a tall dark queenly figure in black and gold with a crown of thorns of shadow, the starless night itself around her; at her side, the knight in dark teal armor with the cold blue blade. The moon above is going out, a thin pale ring.
```

**9. The stars come back** (`still-ending.png`). Attach the Night square backdrop and Lunara's sheet.

```text
The village of Wickhollow at dawn's edge on the longest night: the sky above is full of stars again, thick and bright, and a pale full moon. Golden sparks of burned letters rise into the sky and become stars. Pale moths drift up from the well in the square. The witch Io and the swordswoman Sol sit on the well's rim, looking up.
```
