# Art Request 11: The Battle Backgrounds' Missing Places

For the new battles (pass three, October 3, 2026): 3D ground near the camera, with a painting of the place far off behind it, as in **Colossus in the Meadow**. Chris's combat backgrounds pack (Parts 1 and 2) gave ten paintings; they are kept as they came in `../../reference/art/battle-backgrounds/`, with the packs' notes and prompts in `packs/`.

**Done (October 3, late evening):** all four new paintings arrived (11 to 14) and are kept as they came beside the first ten, with their pack's notes, prompts and manifest in `packs/part-3/`. Every fight has its painting now. Chris's squeeze for them is "Strong" (AVIF quality 20, full size): the eight the game uses take 0.24 MB together.

Matched to where each fight happens on the world map (the game's places on the D&D map: `design-decisions.md`, "Place names").

## Making the four new ones

Take these back to the instance that made the pack, with **the same three reference images** it used (the camera and layout guide, the approved Colossus painting, and the Magpie world map). ## 11: Warm Roads Moorland

**For:** Band 2 wilds, levels 6 to 10. **File name:** `11-verdant-wilds-warm-roads-moor.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production background painting for a fantasy JRPG combat stage in the user's Moonlight in the Aether project.
Output exactly ONE separate landscape 4:3 image, ideally 2048x1536 or larger at 4:3. This is an addition to the same coordinated art pack (images 11 to 14), made with the same three reference images. No collage, contact sheet, captions, borders, UI, characters or monsters.
Reference roles: Image 1 is the camera/layout guide, ONLY for composition and major boundaries. Image 2 is the user's APPROVED combat painting: match its exquisite hand-painted landscape craft, believable scale, lunar lighting, painterly but detailed surfaces, silver-plum-indigo atmosphere and production finish. Image 3 is the Aethermoor WORLD MAP, ONLY for geographical and regional identity; change the viewpoint to a ground-level, distant-horizon battle landscape.
All pack images share the same near-eye-level camera, slight upward angle, 38-degree lens feel, the low geometric horizon approximately 61% down, and a flat, spacious combat clearing covering the bottom third. Keep the distant region's landforms in the guide's mountain bands, primarily 22% to 44% down, and its near landscape band approximately 42% to 67% down; interpret that band as appropriate trees, ruins, desert cliff faces or island/coastal forms. Keep distant forms behind the arena. Put one detailed bright near-full moon at exactly 31% of width and 17% of height, diameter about 13% of image height, with soft lunar bloom. Broad starless plum-indigo night sky. This is before the stars return in the game's story. Use atmospheric silver-lavender haze to separate layers and support dark actors' readability.
The lower third must be entirely level unobstructed land, restrained in contrast, with the scene's natural surface detail decreasing in scale toward the horizon. The game places actors and animated grass/effects here. No foreground rocks, water, steep terrain, steps, huge roots, buildings, props or paths through the center. Any water is confined beyond the playable clearing. No large foreground framing trees: the engine adds its own live trees. No baked lightning, heavy rain, red wrath tint, heavy storm clouds, particles or magical combat effects: those are live in the engine. Preserve a calm base night atmosphere with only thin translucent wisps.
Make each location immediately distinctive, majestic, richly textured and deeply atmospheric, with all regional landmarks at middle/far distance, silhouettes within the layout bands and a quiet open foreground. Avoid excessive micro-detail noise or repeated cloned plants; favor composed painterly forms with fine detail that holds up to camera crops. Paint only the new scene described below; the location reference never means reproduce the map's top-down viewpoint or its labels.
SCENE-SPECIFIC DIRECTION:
A broad level clearing of short moor grass beside the Warm Roads, in the western forests and riverlands of the Verdant Wilds, west of Thornhollow on the world map. Beyond the clearing, an ancient paved road of broad worn flagstones curves away across rolling heather moorland, with fine amber veins of sunstone glowing faintly under its cracks; at the road's far bend stands a squat stone waymarker, an Ember Line node, its crystal gone dim and cold. Gorse, heather, low dry-stone walls and a few birches fill the middle-distance band; beyond them a silver river winds through dark western forest, and a round stone tower stands on a far hilltop; distant mountains keep the layout skyline. The foreground lower third is an entirely level, restrained short heather-and-grass moor, with no road or wall crossing it. Faint warm amber threads against a cool moonlit moor.
```
