# Lore Amendment: Noctara the Starless

**Moonlight in the Aether** · Amends the Lore & Party Bible · October 1, 2026

Where this amendment and the Bible disagree, this amendment wins.

## Summary

The big bad is now **Noctara the Starless**, a goddess of darkness and cold. She replaces the Drowned Mother as the final boss. Halcyon fights beside her as her knight.

## What changes

| In the Bible | Now |
|---|---|
| Final boss: the Drowned Mother | Final boss: Noctara the Starless |
| Theme: drowning, grief, black water | Theme: darkness and cold |
| Window lantern with soul windows (Steal Light, Cradle, Let Go) | Removed. She has no lantern. |
| Hair Lash | Removed. She has no hair moves. |
| Halcyon is fought alone, before the final boss | Halcyon also fights in the final battle, at Noctara's side |

## Who she is

Noctara is the opposite of the two powers the Witch can call on. Lunara is moonlight. The sky-lanterns are prayers, hopes and dreams rising into the night. Noctara is the sky with no light in it at all, which is where "the Starless" comes from.

Her elements are **darkness** and **cold**. Her color is deep purple, which keeps her apart from the Wraith's green.

Halcyon is her cold half. "Halcyon days" are the calm days of midwinter, and the knight's teal and dark armor already fits.

## Her moves

### Blackout

The whole screen goes dark. Noctara stays visible, lit only along her edges. In the dark, Halcyon attacks: all the player sees is a cold blue blade and cold blue eyes. Then the light comes back and the damage lands.

### Void Sphere

A black sphere with a deep purple glow, with particles swirling into it. It opens over the party, collapses, and hits everyone inside.

### Frost Dust

Frost drifts across the whole screen and the game slows down. As it speeds back up, the party takes damage.

Tuning, to be settled in the demo:

- **Who slows.** Only the party slows; Noctara and Halcyon keep normal speed. If everything slows equally, the fight gets longer without getting harder.
- **How long.** Start at 10 to 12 seconds. The first idea was 15 to 20, which may drag. The demo has a slider from 6 to 20 seconds, set to 10.

### Rules for any future move

- No hair moves. Hair can't be animated well enough to carry an attack.
- No lantern.

## Her look

These rules exist so she reads from the battle camera and so Blackout works.

- **Simple hair.** Short, tied back, or under a veil or hood that moves as one shape. No long loose strands.
- **A glowing edge or starlight specks.** A dark figure vanishes on a dark screen, and Blackout depends on her staying visible.
- **Big, simple shapes.** A wide cloak, large sleeves or a long train. Small details get lost.
- **Standard sheet layout.** Front, side, back and three-quarter views, arms slightly out.

Current demo build: 1.9 m tall with her crown, a violet edge light on every surface, and robes, sleeves, cape and veil that move as large shapes. Besides the three moves above, the demo has a basic attack (Crown Shards), Appear, Die, Hurt, Block and a glide.

## Retired

- The name "the Drowned Mother" for the final boss
- The window lantern and its soul windows
- Steal Light, Cradle and Let Go
- Hair Lash

## Still to decide

1. **Her story.** "The central mystery" is written for the Mother: the flood at Misthollow, the witch who would not let her children's souls go, the lit windows. Does any of it carry over to Noctara, or does she get a new origin?
2. **Halcyon's timeline.** The Bible has Halcyon as the Tier 3 boss at level 20, already gone under the black water before the final fight. She now appears in the final battle, so one of those has to change.
3. **Halcyon's colors.** Blackout shows a cold blue blade and eyes. The Bible gives Halcyon a black greatsword with a violet edge and one gold eye. Is blue a Blackout-only effect or a redesign?
4. **Halcyon's pronouns.** The Bible says "she" (Sol's teacher, Kestrel), and this amendment follows it. Recent notes say "he."
5. **Water moves and mechanics.** Black Tide, the plunge, Summon Counterfeit, Soaked and the Tide state all belong to the drowning theme.
6. **Eclipse and the black sun.** It fits darkness and is already the sign in the Wraith's Eclipse attack. Keep it for Noctara?
7. **The ending and Keeper's Word.** Both depended on the lantern and on the Mother being a former Moonwell-keeper.
8. **Place names.** Mother's Hollow and Drowned Misthollow.
9. **Stats.** The Mother's 72,000 HP at level 22 has not been confirmed for Noctara.
10. **Code names.** Any remaining `makeMother` and `mother.js` references become `makeNoctara` and `noctara.js`.
