# Lore and Story Answers

**Moonlight in the Aether** · Answers to `Lore and Story Questions` · October 1, 2026 · Decided by Chris

Numbers match the questions file. Where this disagrees with the Lore & Party Bible or the Noctara amendment, this wins.

## The story in five lines

1. Noctara the Starless is the dark that was there before Auros, the Moon, woke. Souls that die without light become hers; that is what wraiths are.
2. The longest night is when she is strongest. This year she means to eclipse Auros so the night never ends.
3. The Witch and Sol have until the longest night to stop her. Halcyon, Sol's old teacher, stands in the way as Noctara's knight.
4. Midway, the Witch folds the letters to the dead she could never burn into Envoi, and Sol lights its heart.
5. At the end, Envoi's last strike burns the letters for good. Their light goes up and the sky gets its stars back.

**The main cast is all women:** the Witch, Sol, Halcyon, Noctara and Lunara.

## Halcyon

1. **A woman.** She stays Dame Halcyon, "she" everywhere. The finished model keeps its face and body. Any "he" in notes or code comments is a mistake to correct.
2. **Still Sol's teacher.** The Bible backstory stays: she trained Sol, named her Kestrel, went north eight years ago and never came back. At the ambush, Sol steps in front of the Witch, Halcyon sees her old squire and leaves. Sol recognizes the stance, not the face. The reveal that the knight is Halcyon and serves Noctara comes later.
3. **Three fights.** The ambush (unwinnable), one winnable fight around level 20 using the Gloam Knight moves already in the Bible, and the final battle at Noctara's side. At the end of the level 20 fight she retreats into the dark. She is not pulled under black water.
4. **Recolor to cold blue.** Blade edge and both eyes are cold blue at all times, not only during Blackout. The gold eye and the violet blade veins are removed. This is a material change, not a rebuild. Teal and dark armor stay.
5. **She was tired of burning.** Sunsteel burns the hand that holds it. Noctara offered cold and calm, and Halcyon took it. Her sword went black and cold. The lit window and the little brother are retired.

## Noctara

6. **The sky before the Moon.** She wants it back. No tragic backstory.
7. **The story builds toward the longest night.** She does not cause it. She plans to make it permanent.
8. **Keep the eclipse.** The black sun is her sign and her plan. The wraith's Eclipse attack stays as is.
9. **Final battle: the dead Moonwell at Misthollow,** in the north. Misthollow keeps its name as the town whose Moonwell went dark. "Drowned Misthollow" and the flood are retired.
10. **Ending.** Envoi's last strike in the final battle is the real sending: the letters burn for good, their light rises, and the stars come back. Noctara is not killed. She becomes night with stars in it. Halcyon's blade warms, and she goes home as a moth rising.

## Envoi

11. **Made at the third stop,** the Warden waystation, at a living sunstone node. The Witch admits she never burned the letters, folds them into the wyrm, and Sol lights the heart at the node.
12. **Battle rules.**
    - Available in every battle from that story point on, once per battle. No longer limited to one place.
    - Sol must be standing with 70 or more Heat. Summoning spends all of it.
    - Folding Ward still takes the next enemy attack completely.
    - Damage keeps the level curve, so it grows as the Witch levels.
    - Against Noctara: while the ward is up, Blackout cannot be used, and Envoi's strike clears the Frost Dust slow.
    - The "strips the Mother's stolen lights" effect is removed.
    - Between battles it refolds. "A letter can only be sent once" now applies only to the ending.

17. **One mid-game boss at Bogmire:** a scaled-up wraith that has eaten the town's lights. It reuses the wraith model. No other new foes.

## The Witch and the party

19. **Her name is still open.** Keep "the Witch" in every build until Chris picks one. Candidates: Yarrow, Ione, Tansy.
20. **Nettie and Inkblot are not party members in this story.** They stay as characters. The party is the Witch and Sol.

## Tone

21. **Defeat is release, not death.** A beaten wraith or wisp lets a pale moth rise: the soul going home. The Witch is left down, not dead, at the ambush. Nobody dies on screen except Halcyon at the very end, shown as a moth rising.

## What this changes in the builds

- **Halcyon model:** swap the gold eye and violet blade veins for cold blue. Nothing else.
- **Halcyon level 20 fight:** replace the black-water exit with a retreat into the dark. The Kestrel story command stays.
- **Envoi battle code:** drop the Mother's Hollow check and use a story flag instead. Remove the stolen-lights clear. Add the Blackout lockout while `B.envoiWard` is true and the Frost Dust clear on the strike.
- **Enemy ladder:** Old Snuff is replaced by the great wraith at tier 2. Add wisps below the wraith.
- **Names:** Mother's Hollow and Drowned Misthollow are retired. Misthollow stays.
