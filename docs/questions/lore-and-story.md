# Lore and Story Questions

For Chris to take to the lore conversation. Each one blocks some part of the build. The first ten come from the Noctara amendment's "Still to decide" list; the rest are new.

## From the Noctara amendment

1. **Noctara's story.** The bible's central mystery is written for the Drowned Mother: the flood at Misthollow, the keeper who wouldn't let her children go, the lit windows. Does any of it carry over to Noctara, or does she get a new origin?
2. **Halcyon's timeline.** The bible has her as the tier 3 boss, pulled under the black water before the final fight. The amendment puts her at Noctara's side in the final battle. Which is it, or does she fight twice?
3. **Halcyon's colors.** The new Halcyon model has the bible's look: one glowing gold eye and a black blade with violet veins. The Blackout art shows a cold blue blade and blue eyes. Is the blue only during Blackout, or a redesign?
4. **Halcyon's pronouns.** The bible and the model say "she." Some recent notes say "he."
5. **The water moves.** Black Tide, the plunge, Summon Counterfeit, Soaked and the Tide state all belong to the drowning theme. Do any of them stay?
6. **The eclipse.** The black sun is in the Wraith's Eclipse attack. Keep it as Noctara's sign?
7. **The ending.** The ending and Keeper's Word depended on the Mother's lantern and on her being a former Moonwell keeper.
8. **Place names.** Mother's Hollow and Drowned Misthollow were the Mother's places. Where is Noctara's final fight?
9. **Noctara's stats.** Does the Mother's 72,000 HP at level 22 carry over to her?
10. **Code names.** Any leftover `mother` names become `noctara`. (No answer needed; noted for the build.)

## New

11. **Envoi's story role.** The game is named after Envoi. In the bible it is made once, in the final fight, from the letters to the dead, and its big hit also strips the Mother's stolen lights. Against Noctara:
    - What does Envoi's special effect do?
    - Is it still final-fight only, or does the Witch get it earlier and use it in other battles?
12. **The longest night.** Is the story set on (or building toward) the longest night of the year, when keepers burn the letters? Does Noctara cause it, or come with it?
13. **Who is the Drowned Mother now?** Is she gone from the story, kept as one of Noctara's servants, or someone else?
14. **The rest of the enemy ladder.** Are Old Snuff (tier 2) and the mandrakes still in the game? What other foes stand between the wraiths and Halcyon?
15. **The opening.** The plan is the Witch alone against a wraith, then more wraiths, then Sol. Does the first fight stay in the Night square, and does Sol still arrive at the end of it, as in the bible?
16. **The Magpie.** How does the Witch get the ship, and at what point in the story can she fly?
17. **The world.** Which places are in the game, in what order, and how long should it be (number of areas and rough hours)?
18. **The Witch's name.** Every build calls her the Witch.
19. **More party members.** The bible mentions Nettie and Inkblot as a possible third slot. In or out?
20. **The Shadow Wraith's color.** The pose sheet is violet; the battle build and the Envoi art are soul-green, and the amendment keeps the wraith green to stand apart from Noctara's purple. Green?
21. **Tone.** Other versions of this world say "nobody dies" and have no villains. This game has a goddess of darkness and a fallen knight. How dark can it get? Can anyone die on screen?
