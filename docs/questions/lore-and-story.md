# Lore and Story Questions

For Chris to take to the lore conversation. Updated October 1, 2026, after the second round of decisions (see `../design-decisions.md`).

## Already settled

These need no answer. They are listed so the lore conversation builds on them.

- The Drowned Mother, Old Snuff and the mandrakes are gone, with their lore: the flood, the lit windows, the stolen lights, Mother's Hollow, the lantern and the water moves.
- Noctara the Starless, goddess of darkness and cold, is the big bad. Halcyon is her knight.
- The story opens with the Witch alone against a Shadow Wraith. More wraiths follow, then Sol joins.
- Early on, the knight ambushes the Witch in a fight she can't win. He leaves before finishing her. Later the story reveals he serves Noctara.
- Lunara and Envoi are the Witch's two summons. Envoi is a mid-game reward that grows as she levels, and it has to be crazy powerful.
- The Witch gets the Magpie near the beginning. It flies between about four locations, with wilderness between them to explore. Walking there is possible but slow.
- The story should be straightforward and not too long.

## Halcyon

1. **Man or woman?** Chris now calls the knight "he." The bible, Sol's backstory ("Dame Halcyon") and the finished Halcyon model are a woman. If he is a man, the touch-up becomes a rebuild of the face and body.
2. **Is Halcyon still Sol's old teacher?** The bible's backstory: Halcyon trained Sol, named her Kestrel, went north eight years ago and never came back. Does that stay, and does Sol recognize the knight at the ambush?
3. **How many times does the party fight him?** The ambush (unwinnable) and the final battle at Noctara's side are settled. Is there a real, winnable fight against him in between?
4. **His colors.** The model has one gold eye and a black blade with violet veins. The Blackout art shows a cold blue blade and blue eyes. Blue only during Blackout, or a redesign?
5. **Why serve Noctara?** The bible's reason (a lit window holding his brother's soul) belonged to the Drowned Mother and is gone.

## Noctara

6. **Who is she and what does she want?** The bible's central mystery was the Drowned Mother's. Noctara needs her own origin and goal.
7. **The longest night.** The title comes from the custom of burning letters to the dead on the longest night. Is the story set on, or building toward, the longest night of the year? Does Noctara cause it?
8. **The eclipse.** The black sun is already in the wraith's Eclipse attack. Keep it as Noctara's sign?
9. **Where is the final battle?**
10. **How does it end?** The old ending depended on the Mother's lantern.

## Envoi

11. **How does the Witch get Envoi?** The bible has her fold the letters to the dead into the wyrm, with Sol lighting its heart. What moment in the middle of the story is that?
12. **How does it work in battle now?** In the bible it costs all of Sol's Heat, blocks the next enemy attack, then strikes. Its special effect stripped the Mother's stolen lights, which are gone. Keep the Heat cost and the block? What does it do that makes it feel crazy powerful against Noctara?

## The world

13. **The four places.** What are the airship's four destinations, and in what order does the story visit them? Wickhollow (the Witch's home and the Night square) is presumably one.
14. **The wilderness.** What can be found out there: hidden places, items, side fights, people?
15. **How the Witch gets the Magpie.** In 20-min, Quill gives her his old skiff. Does that stay?

## Enemies

16. **The lower-level foe.** Something weaker than a wraith for the early fights and the wilderness. Ideas from the bible that are not retired: the wisps (souls gone sour from waiting), glowcaps, or a creature of Noctara's cold. Or something new.
17. **Other foes.** Besides wraiths, the new foe, Halcyon and Noctara, is anything else needed, such as a mid-game boss?
18. **The wraith's color.** The pose sheet is violet. The battle model and the Envoi art are soul-green, and the amendment keeps the wraith green so it stands apart from Noctara's purple. Green?

## The Witch and the party

19. **The Witch's name.** Every build calls her "the Witch."
20. **Nettie and Inkblot.** The bible floats them as a later third party slot. In or out?

## Tone

21. **How dark can it get?** Other versions of this world say "nobody dies" and have no villains. This one has a goddess of darkness and a knight who nearly kills the Witch. Can anyone die on screen?
