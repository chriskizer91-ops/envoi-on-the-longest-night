## Lore questions

For the separate lore conversation.

3. **What the money is.** Fights give money, and the Magpie's upgrades cost it. What is it in the world, and why does a fight give it? Two ideas:
   - coins, as bounties the towns pay for driving off wraiths; or
   - sunstone shards, left where a soul is released, which is also what the Magpie's lift runs on.
4. **Envoi and the Dawnroost fight.** The largest group of stronger wraiths so far attacks Dawnroost's living node at the level 10 gate. Is Envoi made before that fight, and summoned in it, or after it, as the reward?
