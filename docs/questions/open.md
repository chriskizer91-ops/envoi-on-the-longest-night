## Halcyon

2. **Can the level 15 fight be won?** Halcyon is level 20 there, and Chris expects the party to lose. Should it end in defeat no matter how strong the party is, so the story goes on as after the ambush? Or can a party that has leveled well past 15 win it? Either way she retreats into the dark at the end, and Sol learns Kestrel Stoop.
