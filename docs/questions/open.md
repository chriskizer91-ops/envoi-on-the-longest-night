## Lore questions

For the separate lore conversation.

3. **The Aurosi and the living Moon.** In this game's lore, Auros is the Moon, alive and asleep; its light pools into the Aether, and Lunara carries souls up to it. In Chris's D&D world (the Thareia compendium), Auros is a small world with one-sixth gravity, covered in sunstone, where the Aurosi elves and gnomes live. Are they the same Auros? Some ways it could fit:
   - the Aurosi live on the sleeping Moon's surface, and its heartbeat is the hum of its sunstone;
   - the Aurosi come from a second, smaller moon; or
   - another way.
4. **Gnomes at the shipyard.** In the compendium, Aurosi elves design ships and Aurosi gnomes build them ("elven theory, gnomish application"). Should the shipyard have gnomish hands working under the elven shipmaster?
