# Questions for Chris: Envoi with the Moon Faire witches

November 16, 2026. For the new game in `moon-faire-witches/`, where Moon Faire's Io plays Io and the Jester completely
replaces Sol. Answer them in the lore conversation; none of them stops the other work.

1. **Halcyon without Sol.** In Envoi, Halcyon is the knight Sol knew as Kestrel. She serves Noctara, and in the finale she
   holds back because Sol called her by that name. With the Jester in Sol's place, what is Halcyon to the party? For
   example: the Jester knew her before (fate, life and death); Io reaches her instead; or nobody does, and she doesn't
   hold back.
2. **Halcyon's weakness.** Halcyon is weak to Sol's sun, and nothing in the new party is sun. Should one of the witches get
   a sun move, or should Halcyon be weak to something the witches have (the moon, growing things, Fate)?
3. **The finale and the final boss.** You chose Moon Faire's big bad as the final boss. It is set at the dead Moonwell
   where Noctara was fought, as her black sun (her sign and her plan in Envoi's lore). Is Noctara still fought first, with
   the black sun after her, or does the black sun take her fight's place?
4. **The Jester's name.** She has none in Moon Faire. Does she stay "the Jester" in Envoi too?

## Chris's answers (November 16, morning)

- **1 and 2, Halcyon:** she is replaced by a new Halcyon: Moon Faire's Io's body (the light model) in the paladin outfit from
  Moon Faire's Io outfits model, with her face repainted and a glowing aura made in code. Still a woman. What she is to the
  party, and what she is weak to, come with the new lore (below).
- **3, the finale:** Noctara, the Queen of Darkness, is replaced by Moon Faire's big bad's 3D model, so darkness can mean
  something different in this game.
- **4, the Jester's name:** she stays "the Jester" until Chris names her. If a moon's name has to do with the duality of
  life and death and fate, that should be her name. Candidates, both moons of Jupiter like Io: **Ananke** (Jupiter XII),
  named for the mother of the Fates, who spin a life and cut it; and **Kore** (Jupiter XLIX), another name for Persephone,
  who lives half the year above and half below as queen of the dead.
- **New lore:** the townsfolk's conversations started from Envoi's lore, which is wrong for this game. It is like making
  another game in the same places: an alternate universe. The lore is to be written new (the board's `new-lore` job).
