# Gloamwing

A great night-flier of the marshes that hunts the pale moths that are souls on their way to the Moon: about 3 m tall standing and 9 m from wingtip to wingtip, with a heart-shaped barn owl's face, smoky brown down, bat-like wings that glow amber where light shines through them, and under its chest a glowing sac like a small moon where the souls it has swallowed flutter. Beaten, it lets them all go (from art request 03, `../../docs/art-requests/03-gloamwing-and-emberback.md`, and the Lore & Party Bible's Hollow). Nothing here is canon until Chris places it.
