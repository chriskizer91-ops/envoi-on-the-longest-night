The Bramble Colossus

The Bramble Horror grown as big as a house: the last band’s great wild foe, met rarely and always alone, beside the frozen road in the northeast peaks. Its heart is its weak point: while its flower is open, every blow lands double. At half its HP comes its Wrath. It fears fire, a heavy blade can cut its great canes, and since it is rooted, the party can always flee. Pick the party’s level.
