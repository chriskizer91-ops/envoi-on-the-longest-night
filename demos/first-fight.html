The Shadow Wraith

A Shadow Wraith has crossed the bridge from the Thornwood, drawn to the Moonwell at the heart of the square. When Io’s turn gauge fills, choose a command. Taking hits builds her Trance for Moonlight, and once a battle she can call Lunara, the Pale Mother, up from the well. Choose badly and Io can fall.
