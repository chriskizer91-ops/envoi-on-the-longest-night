The Bramble Horror

What looks like a lush, unusually fruitful blackberry thicket by the Thornwood bridge is a predator: rooted, patient and hungry. It is always met alone, and it is the most dangerous thing in the wilds. It fears fire, and a heavy blade can cut its canes. Pick the party’s level and its form; it comes at the party’s own level.
