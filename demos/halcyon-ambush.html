Halcyon’s Ambush

On the road north to the shipyard, the four old roads meet at a ruined crossroads, and someone is waiting there in dark armor. This is the gate out of the third band, and it isn’t meant to be won at level 15: the knight is level 20. It ends when the party falls, or, if they bring her down to a fifth of her strength, when she retreats. Either way the story goes on.
