Secret · Moon Crest

Moonsteel’s moon edge goes onto Solar Crest. Against a wraith at 100 Heat that’s 1,200 × 1.35 × 1.5 = 2,430 base, more than a Moonlight. And Moonsteel itself gives 40 of the Heat.

Tip · Sunder first

Sundered stacks with everything: weakness, Sunburn, the Lily, the Colossus’s bare heart. Sundered, Sunburned, sun-weak Halcyon takes about 2.5× from a Flare Cut.

Tip · Last to hit

Soul Reaper, Strike and Thorn Lance go after whoever hit that foe last (summons count as Io). Make that Sol: more HP, and +10 Heat from every hit she takes.

Secret · No Moth Veil

The game files still hold the full effect for an Io spell called Moth Veil, but Harvest Moon took its place in the battle rules. It never appears in a menu.
