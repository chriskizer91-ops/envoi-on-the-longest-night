What’s new in the final pass

The earlier guide was written against a build from the small hours of October 5. By that evening the game had new roads, closed roads, new settings and 35 fixes. Here’s what that means for you.

P. 4

The big changes

New The world map is flown, never walked

Nobody walks the world map any more. It’s only for flying the Magpie. In its place, eight new walking maps join each band’s landing camp to its town, so the game now has 21 walking maps (it had 13):

  • Band 2: the Warm Roads camp → the Ember Line road → the forest road to Dawnroost.
  • Band 3: the northern camp → Eldergrove’s edge → the cold moor → the northern crossroads.
  • Band 4: the frozen camp → Frostmere’s shore → the frozen pass.

The camps are proper maps now, each with a fire to rest at and a landing ground where the Magpie glows. There are no fights in camps.

Moved The Ember Line nodes

The three dark nodes are on the Ember Line road now: one in a ring of standing stones up the west side path, one beside the road by the bridge, one on a rise at the end of the east side path. Each still pays 300 shards, and the golden arrow still ignores them.

Closed Roads that turn you back

The cottage’s south road, west footbridge and east forest path all turn Io back now. So do the crossroads’ south and east roads. You reach the crossroads from the west, off the cold moor.

Gone The old shortcuts

You can no longer walk the world map straight to Bogmire or Misthollow. Bogmire is reached through the Thornwood, and Misthollow only through the frozen pass.

Fixed in the final pass
  • Io arrives fully on screen at every map edge, facing into the map.
  • No surprise wild fight a step after Halcyon’s ambush.
  • The arrow pad hides while people talk or the menu is open.
  • Moonlore from the menu says “No one needs it.” instead of wasting MP on full heroes.
  • Taking a keepsake off and on no longer refills HP or MP.
  • Esc closes the menu, the herb shop and the save-code boxes.
  • Battle menus have bigger rows for thumbs, damage numbers stack instead of overlapping, and the end card shows the fight’s frame rate.
  • Envoi’s “Last Word” now always plays when its strike ends a Frost Dust.
  • A hero who starts a fight knocked out kneels instead of standing at 0 HP.
New setting

Battle sharpness

Full, 3/4 (default) or Half, beside Battle frame rate. Drop it to Half if fights stutter on an older phone.

Try-page ideas: on

Four extras in this copy

Footsteps (Soft steps, Cloak’s swish or None), Words move on (By themselves or On a tap), a door sound between maps, and a Buy 10 button in every herb shop.

New look

3D arenas and weather

Every fight but the first is fought in a 3D arena for its region. Wild fights roll rain or a storm one time in four. It’s only looks: weather never changes a number.

Basic training

Envoi plays in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You walk painted towns and wilds, talk to everyone, and fight in a turn-gauge battle system in the spirit of the late PlayStation classics.

P. 6

The title screen

  • Continue appears once you have a save. It loads the slot you saved most recently and puts Io exactly where she stood. The line under it names the slot, level, time played and place.
  • New game plays the prologue and wakes Io in her cottage. It only asks for a slot if one is already in use.
  • Chapters is a level select, and it’s always there. Each chapter starts you at the right level with the shards for the next upgrade and 3 of every herb, but no keepsakes.
  • Load lists your three slots and has Paste a save code for a game copied from another device.

A tap on a person or glowing spot walks Io there and uses it. Taps follow narrow ways too, secret paths included. Walk into a corner and she slips round it. Keep walking and she breaks into a run after about a second.

Tip

Follow the golden arrow. It always points at the next story goal: across a map, out of the right exit, or to the Magpie. It never points at keepsakes, letters or the Ember Line nodes, so look around.

Tip

Tap the mini-map to make it big. It shows exits in blue, people as gold dots, your goal ringed in gold and Io as a pink dot.

Saving, resting, losing

The game saves itself every time you change maps, rest, finish a fight, buy herbs, pick up a keepsake, read a letter, light a node or buy an upgrade. Menu → Save saves too.

Rest points are all free. They refill HP and MP, save, and become the place you wake if you lose. There are ten rest spots: Io’s bed, Wickhollow’s Moonwell, Bogmire’s Lanternless Inn, Dawnroost’s Warden hall, the shipyard’s bunkhouse, the sled camp in the frozen pass, Misthollow’s Lantern House, and the fires at the Warm Roads camp, the northern camp and the frozen camp. Tobb, Marta, Old Gil and Ede will also put you up if you talk to them.

There’s no Game Over. If the whole party falls, you wake at the last place you rested with full HP and MP, and the game gives back every herb you used in that fight. You lose the walk back and the fight’s experience. One exception: Halcyon’s ambush at the crossroads (see Walkthrough III).

Save codes carry a game to another device. Menu → Saves → Save code → Copy. The code starts ENVOI1:. On the other device: Load → Paste a save code, then pick a slot.

The menu

  • Party: level, experience to the next, shards (the only money), each hero’s HP and MP, and what her keepsakes add.
  • Herbs: use herbs outside fights, as many as you like.
  • Items: the keepsakes each hero wears. Tap one for its card and to hand it over.
  • Moonlore: cast Lunar Mend and Waxing Light out of battle.
  • Saves: three slots, plus the save code.
  • Settings: shared by all three slots.
