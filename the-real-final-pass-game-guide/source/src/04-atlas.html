The atlas & the Magpie

The world is one painted continent split into four bands. Each band’s wild foes come in a fixed level range whatever your level, and cold mist keeps the Magpie out of any band she can’t reach yet.

The Magpie’s three upgrades

Each band after the first costs an upgrade. You need the level and the shards; if you’re short, the shipwright tells you which.

Each upgrade moors the Magpie where you bought it. Levels are the only thing the story ever checks: no gate fight locks you out by level.

Flying

Use the Magpie where she’s moored and answer Fly to “Take the Magpie up?” Tap the map to fly to a point, or steer: ▲ faster, ▼ slower, ◀ ▶ to turn. The + and − buttons zoom. Get close to a lit stop and a Land here card appears (Enter or Space presses it). Land where we took off sits top left the whole flight.

No fights happen in the air. The plate top left names the region below: over the Gloamwood, the Gloomfen, the Verdant Wilds, the northern wilds, the Ironspire Peaks, or the open sea.

Caution · Mist

Fly into a band you haven’t opened: “Cold mist: the Magpie needs more lift to fly into it.” Fly toward the central island, the east or the south-east: “Cold mist that never lifts: the Magpie turns back.” Those places stay shut all game.

Tip · Walk the rows

Every band past the first is a row of walking maps from camp to town. The hidden count to the next random fight carries from map to map, so a row is one long walk. The camps have no fights: rest at the fire before you set off.
