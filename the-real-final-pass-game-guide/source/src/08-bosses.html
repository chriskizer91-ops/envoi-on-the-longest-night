Shadow Wraith

The first fight · Wickhollow’s Night square · Io alone
Level1
HP4,200
Weak toMoon ×1.5
Reward220 XP · 60◆
Danger
  • Soul Reaper about 218 to whoever hit it last.
  • Soul Bolts three bolts of about 81 at random.
  • Shadow Grasp about 288, always at Io.
  • Eclipse once, below 35% (1,470 HP): about 550 on Io.

The plan

  1. Io starts with her gauge 60% full. Open with Crescent Blades: about 660 a cast against its moon weakness.
  2. When Io nears half of her 1,400 HP, call Lunara. She heals 60%, softens every hit to 60%, and her Silver Requiem lands about 3,000 on a lone wraith.
  3. Finish with Crescent Blades, or Moonlight if Trance comes up. Defend if Io is low as it nears 35%. A Silver Mugwort buys one more Crescent.

The great wraith

Gate 5 · The fen’s dark heart, Bogmire
Level5
HP26,542
Weak toMoon ×1.5
Gauge3.4 s
Reward415 XP · 415◆
Danger
  • Soul Reaper 829 to whoever hit it last.
  • Soul Bolts four bolts of 270 at random.
  • Stolen Fire 705 to both heroes.
  • Swallow Lamplight at or below 70% (18,579 HP): heals itself 1,593, then can’t for 3 turns.
  • Eclipse once, below 35% (9,290 HP): 1,389 to both.

The plan

  1. Moon is everything. Io’s Crescent Blades do about 1,370. Cast Moonsteel on Sol: +40 Heat, and her next Attack lands as moon for about 1,040.
  2. Better still: Moonsteel, then let Sol build to 100 and fire a moon Solar Crest for about 5,000.
  3. Keep the pressure through its heals. Swallow Lamplight can only come every fourth turn.
  4. For the Eclipse, either have Lunara out to cut it to 60%, or burst it from above 9,290 to zero: Lunara (about 6,400) plus Moonlight (about 3,900) does it.
  5. Go in at level 5: a careful player wins 9 times in 10. At level 4 you lose about a third of the time.

Three wraiths at the node

Gate 10 · Dawnroost’s living node
Level12 each
HP19,318 ×3
Weak toMoon ×1.5
Reward891 XP · 669◆
Danger
  • Soul Reaper 1,412 to whoever hit it last.
  • Soul Bolts three bolts of 520.
  • Shadow Grasp 1,858, always at Io.
  • Eclipse once each, below 35% (6,761 HP): 2,675 to both. Three wraiths, three Eclipses.

The plan

  1. One at a time. Pick a wraith and keep hitting it until it’s gone. Nightbloom Briars slows another meanwhile.
  2. Finish each from above 35% in one burst so its Eclipse never fires. At level 10, Moonlight averages about 9,700, Moonfall alone about 8,900, and Moonsteel + Solar Crest at 100 Heat about 12,500. Each is more than the 6,761 line.
  3. Shadow Grasp always goes for Io. When she’s low, have Sol Guard to take it.
  4. Waxing Light and the Lavender cover the spread damage. Save Lunara for when someone drops. Go in at 10 (9 in 10 wins for careful play), or 11 to be safe.

The Gloam Knight (Halcyon)

Gate 15 · The northern crossroads
Level20 always
HP632,570
Weak toSun ×1.5
Retreats at20%
Reward2,556 XP · 3,834◆
Danger but you can’t lose
  • Gloam Cleave 14,377 to one hero.
  • Dusk Arc 10,543 to both.
  • Severance 11,821 to the hero with the lowest HP, who then can’t be healed until her next turn.
  • Light-Drinker 9,105, healing Halcyon; also drains 40 of Sol’s Heat or about 100 of Io’s MP.
  • Warden’s Vow until her next turn, every physical action against her draws an 11,821 counter. Magic doesn’t.
  • Black Noon once, below 30% (189,771 HP). Charged: 31,149 to both.

The plan

  1. You’re fighting for the payout. Being wiped out just means she spares you. Odds for sharp play: level 17, 1 in 8; level 18, 3 in 5; level 19, 9 in 10; level 20, nearly always.
  2. When she takes Warden’s Vow, stop hitting physically, Io’s plain Attack included. Use Io’s spells and summons, or have Sol Guard.
  3. Hit with sun: Flare Cut, High Noon and above all Envoi (about 108,000 at level 18). Sunder her first.
  4. Skip Black Noon: she can’t be killed here, and the fight ends the moment she’s at 20%. If you carry her from above 30% to 20% (63,257 HP) before her next turn, Black Noon never comes. At level 18+, Envoi’s strike alone does it.
  5. If she does start gathering it, have Sol call Kestrel: she loses a turn, but the blow still falls on the turn after. Use that time to push her to 20%, or put up Envoi’s ward or Lunara for it.
  6. Severance targets whoever has the lowest raw HP, usually Io. Heal her before she needs it.

The Bramble Colossus

Rare · Band 4 wilds · Optional
Level16–20
HP262k–543k
Weak toFire ×1.5 · Sun ×1.25
Gauge2.3 s
Reward at L2015,096 XP · 10,903◆
Danger but you can always run
  • Thorn Lance about 17,900 to whoever hit it last (level 20).
  • Hammerfall about 14,500 to one, then a 5,300 shockwave to all.
  • Maelstrom / Thorn Volley about 13,000 / 12,500 to everyone.
  • Thornwood about 21,000 to one.
  • Siren Bloom charged: charms the party to half speed and bares its heart. Next turn, Devour: about 34,700 to one hero, healing itself.
  • Wrath once, at its first turn below 50%: faster, hits 20% harder, and bares its heart.

The plan

  1. Always break the Siren Bloom. Any fire or sun hit does it. Flame Bolt is perfect. So does any single hit of 450+ base on the bare heart: Moonlight’s third hit, Lunara’s Moonfall, Solar Crest.
  2. Hit the heart. While it’s bare, every blow does double, summons included: during the bloom, after a Devour, and after Wrath until its next turn. Save Solar Crest at 100 Heat or High Noon for those windows. Wrath uses its turn, so that window is free.
  3. Cut its canes. Flare Cut, Solar Crest, Kestrel Stoop’s dive and High Noon each sever a cane. Each cane gone takes 8% off its damage, down to two canes.
  4. Nightbloom Briars slows it, fire and sun hits knock its gauge back, and Envoi’s ward can swallow a Devour or a Maelstrom whole.
  5. Going badly? Flee. It always works against the Colossus.

Noctara the Starless & Halcyon

The finale · The dead Moonwell, Misthollow
Level20 & 20
Noctara HP926,492
Halcyon HP253,028
Weak toNothing · Halcyon: sun
Reward2,556 XP · 3,834◆
Danger
  • The Cold every action Noctara takes adds 11.5% to her damage. After 10 actions she hits 2.15× as hard. Halcyon’s grows the same way, though the game doesn’t show it.
  • Crown Shards three shards of about 1,660 at random.
  • Void Sphere charged: about 8,900 to everyone, before the Cold.
  • Frost Dust slows the party for 10 seconds; its hit (about 1,800 each) lands when the frost thaws.
  • Blackout only while Halcyon stands: the screen goes dark and Halcyon strikes out of turn, Gloam Cleave at 1.25×.
  • Halcyon brings her whole move list, Warden’s Vow and Black Noon included, at 35% damage.

The plan

  1. Be level 20. Sharp play wins about 3 in 4 there, and 1 in 20 at level 19.
  2. Take Halcyon down first. She has a quarter of Noctara’s HP, she’s still weak to sun (Envoi alone does about 155,000 to her), and while she stands Noctara can Blackout and Halcyon’s moves can eat Envoi’s ward. When she falls she kneels on her blade and stays down.
  3. Race the Cold. Ember-star Lily on turn one, Sunder Noctara, and spend Trances on damage: Sol’s as Crest, Crest, Crest, High Noon; Io’s for 8-MP party Mends and Moonlight.
  4. Answer every Void Sphere. You get one turn’s warning. Envoi’s ward cancels it (if nothing else uses the ward first), Lunara cuts it to 60% and heals, Defend and Guard halve it, and Kestrel Stoop lifts Sol out of reach. Keep Sol at 70+ Heat with Harvest Moon so Envoi is ready.
  5. The Misthollow Cowl halves Frost Dust’s slow. Envoi’s ward up when Noctara casts it swallows the frost whole; Envoi’s strike ends a frost but brings its hit down at once.

Wisp

  • Flicker: one quick hit.
  • Cling: three small draining hits.
  • Wail: a weak hit on everyone.
  • Gutter: it flickers low, and the next move aimed at it misses. Never twice running.

When a wisp is Guttering, hit it with Sol’s Attack (it still builds +20 Heat when it misses) or turn on its friends. Lunara’s and Envoi’s strikes always hit.

Frost wisp

Like a wisp, but with Frost Breath: a hit on everyone that slows the party’s gauges to 60% for a few seconds. One Flame Bolt does most of the work. The Misthollow Cowl halves the slow for its wearer.

Shadow Wraith

  • Soul Reaper: heavy hit on whoever hit it last.
  • Soul Bolts: three bolts at random.
  • Shadow Grasp: always at Io.
  • Eclipse: once, below 35%, hits everyone.

Two or three wraiths means two or three Eclipses. Finish them one at a time.

Bramble Horror

“A blackberry thicket stands by the road, lusher than it should be, and heavy with fruit.”

One band-3 wild fight in five is a lone Bramble Horror in one of four forms. It fears fire: every fire or sun hit knocks its gauge back 30%.

How often fights come

Random fights happen only on the eight wild walking maps. A hidden counter adds up the distance Io walks there and starts a fight when it passes a gap rolled after each fight. The counter carries across maps, resets after a fight or Halcyon’s ambush, and starts from zero when you reload.
