Secrets & letters

Hidden ways onto rooftops, letters left for the lost, rules buried in the battle code, and a few odd corners nobody explains.

P. 48

The five letter wells

Scattered across the world are letters left for the lost, each with a small gift. They’re optional, but the ending counts them: the credits card shows Letters sent, from 1 (Io’s own bundle) up to 6 if you read all five. Tick them off:

  • The moon stone · the Thornwood · gift: Moonpetal
    “Aldo. The bridge lamp is lit every night now. If you’re still in the woods, come home by it. — your sister.”
  • The water stair · Bogmire · gift: 120 shards
    “Mam. The boardwalks are dark and I’m not scared. I keep your lamp in the window even with no flame in it. — Pell.”
  • The yard well · Dawnroost · gift: Lavender
    “To the Wardens who didn’t come back from the north. We kept your bunks.”
  • The crossroads well · after the ambush · gift: Silver Mugwort
    “To my love at the yard: the cold is coming down the road early this year. Light the lamps. I’ll find my way. — Ennis.”
  • A frozen trough · Misthollow · gift: Nightrose
    “To the Moon. Please come back. — the children of Misthollow.”

Already carrying 99 of a well’s herb? Io trades it on for the local shop price in shards instead. The ending line “Sent. All of them. Even the ones at the wells.” plays whatever your count.

Secret · Paths that look like scenery

If it looks like you could walk there, try it. Keepsakes sit on six of these:

  • Wickhollow’s red roof (Crescent Locket)
  • the Thornwood’s dark south trail (Warden’s Brooch) and north-east trail (Frog-Ring)
  • Bogmire’s north-west porch walk (Hag-Stone)
  • Dawnroost’s wall-walk (Kettle-Helm)
  • the shipyard’s cove pier (Dockhand’s Gloves)
  • Misthollow’s balcony bridge (Misthollow Cowl)

Plenty of others hold nothing (yet): the node’s hidden nook behind the weapon stall, Misthollow’s tower stair, Wickhollow’s lane up the big house’s west side, and every side path on the eight new wilderness maps.

Caution · Before the first fight

Reachable without starting the fight: Nettie, Hilde, the red roof, the jetty (Quill’s herbs and the Jetty Coin). Not reachable: Gretch, the Moonwell rest, the Forge Horseshoe. They’re all inside the fight zone.

Hidden battle rules

  • Heroes win ties. Ready at the same moment as a foe, a hero goes first.
  • No crits, no misses. The only dodges are a Guttering wisp, a hovering Sol and Envoi’s ward. Summon strikes always hit a Guttering wisp.
  • Herbs don’t burn Trance turns. (A herb at 70+ Heat still burns Sol’s HP.)
  • Dealing damage fills Trance only to 99%. The last 1% always comes from taking a hit.
  • A hovering Sol can’t be touched. Even hit-everyone moves skip her while Kestrel Stoop holds her in the air.
  • Envoi is always ready in Dawnbreaker. Sol’s Trance pins her Heat at 100 and Envoi doesn’t drain it. Her old Heat comes back after.
  • Fleeing leaves the fallen down. Only a win gets knocked-out heroes up, at 10% HP.
  • Warden’s Oath: with Io under 25%, Sol takes her single hits at 70%. Guard beats it at 50%.
  • Kestrel Stoop’s dive keeps the heat: rise at 70+ Heat (or as your last Trance action) and the dive still lands at 1.35×.
  • Halcyon can’t die at the crossroads. An overkill blow leaves her at 1 HP, and the fight ends as her retreat.
  • Swallow Lamplight and Consume only happen once the foe is at or below 70%.
  • The finale pays out. Halcyon’s 2,556 XP and 3,834 shards still count when she falls at the Moonwell.

Curiosities

  • Places you can’t reach. The atlas shows a walled castle on an island in the inner sea, and badlands with roads and villages to the east and south-east. Both stay under “cold mist that never lifts.”
  • Who is Inkblot? Quill frets about Inkblot and nobody ever explains. Look at his portrait: it’s the black crow on his shoulder. The Magpie even has a perch for it.
  • Sol’s sword. Hilde admires it; Sol says it was her teacher’s before it was hers. Her teacher was Halcyon.
  • The Moonwell pose. Resting at Wickhollow’s Moonwell is the only rest where Io raises her hands in a casting pose. At wells and keepsakes she kneels.
  • Innkeepers’ toasts use their names: “Rested at Tobb. The game is saved.”
  • Two cutscenes. “The Colossus, first met” (45 s) before your first Colossus, and “The finale’s opening” (72 s). Each plays once a game; Settings → Watch again replays them.
  • No wild fight right after the ambush. Halcyon’s fight resets the count to the next random fight.
  • Never stranded. If a loss or an old save leaves you where you can’t walk to the Magpie, she’s moved to the nearest landing you can reach.
Codes corner

There are no cheat codes. The nearest thing is Chapters on the title: a level select that starts you at any gate with the right level and shards, always on, no unlock needed. It doesn’t give you keepsakes. And a save code (ENVOI1:…) moves a whole game between devices.

Top 30 tips

Everything we wish we’d known on our first night at the Moonwell.

P. 50
  1. Climb the red roof firstThe Crescent Locket is reachable before the first fight. Just tap the glint.
  2. Grab the Jetty Coin early10% off every herb for the whole game, a short walk from Quill.
  3. Wear the Hag-StoneEvery hidden glint shines steadily instead of flickering now and then.
  4. Shop at homeBand-1 shops keep band-1 prices all game. Fly to Wickhollow and hit Buy 10.
  5. Losing is freeYou wake at your last rest with full HP, full MP and every herb back. Try a boss early just to see it.
  6. Except at the crossroadsLose to Halcyon and the story goes on, but the herbs you used are gone.
  7. Tune the fight rateSettings → Random fights: More for grinding, Fewer for exploring.
  8. Rest at the camp firstCamps have no fights. Rest at the fire before every wilderness row.
  9. Light the three nodes900 shards on the Ember Line road. The golden arrow never points at them.
  10. Quill firstThe Thornwood bridge stays shut until Quill hands you the Magpie.
  11. Lunara before, not afterCall her right after a foe starts gathering. The 60% shield lasts only until Io’s next turn.
  12. Harvest Moon into EnvoiOne cast takes Sol from 0 to 70 Heat, exactly what Envoi needs.
  13. Sunder first+25% from the whole party for three of the foe’s turns, stacking with everything.
  14. Spend Heat from 70+Sunburn is checked before the cost is paid. One big art from 70+ and Sol never burns.
  15. Crest, Crest, Crest, NoonIn Dawnbreaker, Solar Crest is free at full 100 Heat. Three of them, then High Noon.
  16. Moon CrestMoonsteel, then Solar Crest at 100 Heat: about 2,430 base against wraiths and wisps.
  17. Trance heals cheapIn a Lunar Trance, Lunar Mend heals the whole party for 8 MP.
  18. Let Sol take the hit“Last attacker” moves go for whoever hit last. Make that Sol.
  19. Guard for IoSol’s Guard pulls every single hit off Io, each Soul Bolt and Crown Shard included.
  20. Read the target listIt’s the only place the game shows enemy HP as a number.
  21. Skip the EclipseCarry a wraith from above 35% to zero in one burst and its Eclipse never fires.
  22. Respect the VowWhen Halcyon takes Warden’s Vow, no physical moves until her next turn, not even Io’s Attack.
  23. Skip Black NoonBurst Halcyon from above 30% to 20% in one go and the crossroads fight just ends.
  24. Kestrel only delaysCalling Halcyon by name costs her one turn. Black Noon still falls after.
  25. Flame Bolt the bloomAny fire or sun hit breaks the Colossus’s Siren Bloom. Then hit the bare heart for double.
  26. Run from rootsFleeing any Bramble Horror or the Colossus always works.
  27. Halcyon first at the MoonwellShe’s still weak to sun, and while she stands Noctara can Blackout and Halcyon can eat Envoi’s ward.
  28. Level 20, no exceptionsThe finale is barely winnable at 19. Frostmere’s shore and the frozen pass are your training ground.
  29. Read every letterThe five wells are quiet and easy to miss, and the credits count them.
  30. Share the shared charms“Either” keepsakes all go to Sol at first. Their HP and damage bonuses only help the wearer, so balance them under Items. Shard, price, glint and flee helps work from either hero.
