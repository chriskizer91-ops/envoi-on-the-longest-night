Walkthrough · Part I · Band 1 · Levels 1–5

Wickhollow to the fen

Foes level 1–5 · Keepsakes: 9 · Letters: 2 · Boss: the great wraith. Three nights before the longest night, the lamps by Wickhollow’s bridge start going out one by one.

P. 19

Io’s cottage

The prologue plays over a painting of the village (“Wickhollow. The longest night of the year is three nights off.”), then the game saves and hands you Io in her cottage yard. Look at the letters on the table: they matter later. The bed is a free rest point and your first save spot.

The south road, west footbridge and east forest path all turn Io back (“Something is wrong up in the square. I should go and see first.”). The only way out is the north lane up to Wickhollow.

Wickhollow

{{map:wickhollow}}

Before the first fight

The south lane runs straight into the square, and stepping south or east of the Moonwell starts the first fight. You don’t have to go straight in. Turn west along the lane under the garden fence and take the west stairs up, and you can reach all of this first:

  • Nettie’s stall (all five herbs). You start with 3 of each, which is plenty.
  • Hilde, along the square’s north edge.
  • The Crescent Locket, hidden on the red roof just south of Nettie’s stall. See the box.
  • The jetty, down the west stairs: Quill’s herb stall and the Jetty Coin.

Mayor Gretch, the Moonwell rest and the Forge Horseshoe’s lane are all inside the fight zone, so they wait.

The first fight

Gretch runs up: “Io! Something came out of the Thornwood.” Io faces a Shadow Wraith alone: 4,200 HP, weak to moon. Open with Crescent Blades (about 660 a cast). When Io is near half HP, call Lunara: she heals 60%, shields you, and her Silver Requiem lands about 3,000 on a lone wraith. Defend if Io is low as it nears 35%. Full plan in the Boss files.

Lose, and Io wakes in her own bed with her herbs back; the wraith waits on the bridge.

Sol arrives

The win pays 220 experience (straight to level 2) and 60 shards. A Warden comes running over the east bridge with her sword burning: Sol, of Dawnroost, the last of the Wardens. She joins for good. Gretch sends you to Quill at the jetty. Io now also has Waxing Light and Moonsteel.

Before you leave

Talk to Nettie again: she gives Io her Knotted Shawl. Then take the lane down the west side of the house nearest the east bridge, past its door and the bench, and east behind the bushes into a leafy nook: the Forge Horseshoe. The Moonwell is now a free rest point.

Secret · Chris’s secret way

The Crescent Locket’s card says it: “Hidden on top of the red roof in Wickhollow, at the end of Chris’s secret way.” It’s one of Io’s two best charms, and you can take it before the first fight.

  1. Stand on the square’s edge just east of the red-roofed house’s tall chimney.
  2. Go south through the tree on the chimney’s east side.
  3. Double back north-west up the roof slope to the ridge.
  4. Follow the ridge south to its very end, above the front gable.

The easy way: just tap the glint. Tap-to-walk follows the secret way by itself in this build.

Io: “Whoever hid this didn’t want it found from the ground.”

The jetty & the Magpie

{{map:jetty}}

Take the west stairs down from the square. Once Sol has joined, talking to Quill plays the Magpie scene: she hasn’t flown in years and the sunstone in her keel has gone cold, but she’ll lift, “not far, mind, and not into the dark.” With Bogmire’s lamps out there’s nowhere to land, so you walk: “The Thornwood road starts at the stone bridge east of the square.” The east bridge opens only now.

Before you go, follow the waterfront west to its far end under the willows for the Jetty Coin: every herb 10% cheaper for the rest of the game.

Tip

Quill sells herbs at the jetty only until Bogmire’s lights come back. After that he moves to Bogmire’s west dock and stops trading. His prices are Nettie’s.

Quill: “Lamps by the bridge are going out. You’d best see to it before Inkblot starts fretting.”

The Thornwood

{{map:thornwood}}

Cross the east bridge. This is the first place with random fights: wisps and Shadow Wraiths, level 1 to 5, up to three at a time. Both are weak to moon, so Crescent Blades is your best friend. Band-1 fights are fought in Wickhollow’s river glade arena.

The moon stone: in the clearing north of the road stands a stone with a crescent cut into it. Someone has left a letter at its foot (“Aldo. The bridge lamp is lit every night now…”) and a Moonpetal.

The north-east trail from the clearing ends at the Thornwood Frog-Ring. Io: “Hello, you.” Three other trails off the clearing (north-west, a short spur north, and east) are empty dead ends.

The dark south trail: east of the bridge the road splits round a thicket. From the lower arm, a faint trail drops south into the dark woods, curves south-east, then runs east along the bottom of the map to a dead end. That’s where the Warden’s Brooch lies, “sunk in the moss among the thorns.” Sol: “I’ll wear it home for them.”

Tip · Level up here

The great wraith is a level 5 boss, and the refit after it needs level 5 and 650 shards. A band-1 fight averages about 123 experience and 94 shards. Set Random fights to More and walk the Thornwood back and forth.

Bogmire

{{map:bogmire}}

“Bogmire. Not one lamp burns on the boardwalks. The townsfolk sit in the dark behind shut doors.”

The whole town is dim until you fix it. Talk to Pell: “It took the lights. All of them. It sits out in the fen’s heart, up the long walk north, with them burning in its ribs.” Old Wenna sells herbs by touch, and Tobb’s Lanternless Inn is a free rest point.

Two keepsakes here. The Hag-Stone: from the west boardwalk climb the stair up to the north-west house’s porch, then go east along the porch and the bridge to the next house; it hangs on a post partway along. The Bogstriders: from the square’s east boardwalk go south down the east side to the south-east dock, and east to its last plank.

The water stair, south of the square: a bucket on a rope with something wrapped in oilcloth. It’s Pell’s letter to Mam, and 120 shards.

Secret · Treasure finder

Glints are tiny and only flare now and then. Put the Hag-Stone on either hero and every hidden keepsake shines steadily and much brighter. Get it early and leave it on.

The fen’s dark heart

{{map:bogmire-heart}}

At level 5, rest at the inn and take the long walk north. Step into the square: “There. In the middle of the square, with Bogmire’s light inside it.” The great wraith rises: level 5, 26,542 HP, weak to moon. Its Eclipse hits both heroes for about 1,400 once it drops below 35%. Plan in the Boss files.

The lights come home. Win, and the stolen lamplight flies back over the water to every window. You’re set down by the inn: Tobb promises you’ll never pay for a bed again, and Quill has flown the Magpie over to the west dock.

Go back up to where the wraith sat. At the top of the square, by the drowned hall’s door, the Fen-Heart Lamp is still burning: “It kept burning all through the dark.”

The refit. Talk to Quill on Bogmire’s west dock. For 650 shards at level 5, Bogmire’s shipwrights fit warm sunstone into the Magpie’s keel. Io learns Harvest Moon (+70 Heat for Sol in one cast), and the Magpie can fly the western forests: band 2.

Tip · Shards for the refit

The great wraith pays 415 shards on its own. Add the water stair’s 120 and a few wild fights and you’ll have the 650 by the time you reach the dock.
