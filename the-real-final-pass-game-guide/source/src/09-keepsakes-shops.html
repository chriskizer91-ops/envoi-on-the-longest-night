Collector’s checklist · All twenty, and where they hide

The keepsakes

Keepsakes are the game’s equipment: small charms with one main help and one extra. Fourteen lie on the maps as a faint “Something glinting,” four are gifts, and two come out of the first Bramble Colossus you fell.

P. 44
  • No slots. Each hero can wear as many as she likes.
  • New finds go on straight away. Io-only and Sol-only charms go to their owner; “either” charms go to Sol once she has joined.
  • Move “either” charms under Menu → Items. Tap one for its card.
  • Nothing can be missed for good. Every keepsake is still there after the ending. Chapters start you with none.
0/20

Tick them off as you go. Your ticks stay on this device only.

Best combos
  • Faster Trance: Io with the Crescent Locket and Pass Bell (+20%); Sol with the Warden’s Brooch and Crossroads Pennant (+20%).
  • Hot start: the Node Sunstone and Ede’s Hearth-Coal put Sol at 20 Heat when every fight begins.
  • Rich run: the Forge Horseshoe and Dockhand’s Gloves add 15% shards to every win.
  • All of Io’s healing charms together add exactly +25% to Lunar Mend and Waxing Light.
  • Sol in everything “either” plus her own: +10% HP and +10% damage.
Secret · Treasure finder

Glints are tiny and only flare every few seconds. The Bogmire Hag-Stone, worn by either hero, makes every hidden keepsake shine steadily and much brighter. Shared helps like this (shards, herb prices, glints, fleeing) count from whoever wears them.

The list, in the order you’ll find them

The Crescent Locket ★

Io · Hidden · Wickhollow · Part I

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 6.25% more. Her Trance fills 10% faster.

On the red roof just south of Nettie’s stall, at the end of Chris’s secret way. Before the first fight if you like.

The Jetty Coin

Either · Found · The jetty · Part I

Her blows and spells land 1% harder. Herbs cost 10% less in shops.

The far west end of the waterfront walk, under the willows. Before the first fight if you like.

The Forge Horseshoe

Either · Found · Wickhollow · Part I

She has 1% more HP. Fights give 5% more shards.

After the first fight: the nook behind the house by the east bridge. Down the lane on its west side, past the door and bench, east behind the bushes.

Nettie’s Knotted Shawl

Io · Gift · Wickhollow · Part I

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 2.5% more. The herbs she uses in a fight heal 10% more.

Talk to Nettie once Sol has joined.

The Thornwood Frog-Ring

Either · Found · The Thornwood · Part I

She has 1% more HP. After each fight she gets back 5% of her HP.

Past the moon stone, at the end of the north-east trail.

The Warden’s Brooch ★

Sol · Hidden · The Thornwood · Part I

She has 2.5% more HP and her blows land 2.5% harder. Her Trance fills 10% faster.

The dead end of the dark south trail, which drops off the road’s lower arm east of the bridge.

The Bogmire Hag-Stone

Either · Found · Bogmire · Part I

Her blows and spells land 1% harder. Hidden things glint brighter on the maps.

Up the north-west house’s stair, east along its porch and bridge. It hangs on a post.

The Bogstriders

Either · Found · Bogmire · Part I

She has 1% more HP. Running from a pack works 2 times in 3, not 1 in 2.

Down the east side from the east boardwalk, at the end of the south-east dock.

The Fen-Heart Lamp

Io · Found · The fen’s dark heart · Part I

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 3% more. After each fight she gets back 5% of her MP.

After the great wraith: the top of the square, by the drowned hall’s door.

The Wardens’ Hall Gauntlets

Sol · Gift · Dawnroost · Part II

Her blows land 1.5% harder. Her Sunder lasts a turn longer.

Talk to Marta at the Warden hall.

The Dawnroost Kettle-Helm

Either · Found · Dawnroost · Part II

She has 1% more HP. The big blows a foe warns of hit her 10% softer.

Just inside the north gate, west along the wall-walk behind the hall, to its end.

The Node Sunstone

Sol · Found · The living node · Part II

Her blows land 1% harder. She starts each fight with 10 Heat.

After the three wraiths: the top of the west stair.

The Crossroads Pennant

Sol · Found · The crossroads · Part III

She has 1% more HP. Her Trance fills 10% faster.

After the ambush: tied to a waymark far down the east road.

Ysmera’s Moonglass

Io · Gift · The shipyard · Part III

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 3.75% more. She has 10% more MP.

Talk to Ysmera again after her first scene.

The Dockhand’s Gloves

Either · Found · The shipyard · Part III

Her blows and spells land 1% harder. Fights give 10% more shards.

West from the bunkhouse yard, down the terrace stairs to the cove, out to the end of the west pier.

The Pass Bell

Io · Found · The frozen pass · Part IV

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 4% more. Her Trance fills 10% faster.

Half buried in snow under the lamp on the west verge, across the road from the sled camp.

Ede’s Hearth-Coal

Sol · Gift · Misthollow · Part IV

She has 1.5% more HP. She starts each fight with 10 Heat.

Talk to Ede at the Lantern House.

The Misthollow Cowl

Either · Found · Misthollow · Part IV

She has 1% more HP. Frost slows her for half as long.

From the upper square’s west end, up the west stairs, then west along the balcony bridge to its end.

The Colossus Heart-Seed

Io · Won in a fight · Band 4 wilds · Part IV

Lunar Mend and Waxing Light heal 5.5% more. At the start of each of her turns she heals 1% of her HP.

Beat your first Bramble Colossus (from the 4th band-4 wild fight on, 1 in 12).

The Colossus Thorn

Sol · Won in a fight · Band 4 wilds · Part IV

Her blows land 2% harder. Kestrel Stoop lands 10% harder.

Comes with the Heart-Seed from the same first win.

Herbs, shops & levels

Every shop in the game is an herb stall, and every one sells the same five herbs for sunstone shards. You can carry 99 of each, but in a fight each kind can only be used once.

HerbDoes

LavenderHeals both for 1.2× a Waxing Light.
Silver MugwortGives Io back 40 MP, more at higher levels (121 at level 20).
Ember-star LilyEvery blow the party lands is 10% harder for the rest of the fight, summons included. Battle only.
NightroseBrings a fallen hero back at 25% HP.

Secret · Save over 90%

A shop charges for its own band, not yours. Nettie and Old Wenna keep band-1 prices for the whole game, about one fifteenth of Sorrel’s. Fly home to Wickhollow, wear the Jetty Coin, and press Buy 10 until you’re at 99 of everything. A Nightrose costs 156 there with the coin, against 2,396 in Misthollow with it.

Grinding plan
  • Level 5 before the great wraith: about 5 or 6 Thornwood fights after the first fight.
  • Level 10 before the node: the Ember Line road and forest road, Random fights on More. Light the 3 nodes for 900 shards.
  • Level 15 for the moon-sail: Eldergrove’s edge and the cold moor. Flee any Ancient Crown above your level.
  • Level 18–19 if you want Halcyon’s 3,834-shard payout.
  • Level 20 for the finale, no exceptions: about 15 band-4 fights from 16.
