Walkthrough · Part IV · Band 4 · Levels 15–20

Over the peaks

Foes level 16–20 · Keepsakes: 2 + a gift + 2 from the Colossus · Letters: 1 · Rare boss: the Bramble Colossus · Final boss: Noctara. “Misthollow is up through the pass. The cold is coming from there.” “Then so is she.”

The frozen camp

{{map:frozen-camp}}

Fly east over the peaks to The northeast peaks. The frozen camp is a rest point. Misthollow has no landing of its own: you always walk up from here, east along Frostmere’s shore and north through the pass. The camp’s snow lane, waterfall trail and lookout rocks are scenery.

Frostmere’s shore New map

{{map:frostmere-shore}}

Band-4 fights start here: frost wisps and wraiths, level 16 to 20, fought on Frostmere’s shore arena (a storm here is a blizzard). The hollow among the rocks and the boathouse jetty out onto the ice are empty. North to the frozen pass.

The frozen pass

{{map:frozen-pass}}

Climb the snowy road north. Just south of the stone bridge, on the east verge, someone’s sled is left with a lamp burning: the sled camp, a free rest point. Right across the road on the west verge, half buried in the snow under a lamp, is the Pass Bell. “It rings once, very softly, as Io lifts it.”

Grind to level 20

This is the step that matters most. The finale cannot be won below level 19, and at 19 it’s close to hopeless. The curve jumps here: level 16 takes 3,257 experience, level 17 takes 10,763. A band-4 fight averages about 3,950 experience, so it takes 3 to 5 fights a level, and about 15 to climb from 16 to 20. Walk Frostmere’s shore and the pass with Random fights on More, and rest at the sled camp.

Caution · The Bramble Colossus

From your 4th band-4 wild fight on, each one has a 1-in-12 chance of being a Bramble Colossus: a thicket as big as a house that heaves itself out of the snow. 262,000 to 543,000 HP. You can always run, because it’s rooted. Beat one once and it leaves two keepsakes in its heart: the Heart-Seed and the Colossus Thorn. It pays up to 15,096 experience, the best haul in the game.

Misthollow

{{map:misthollow}}

“Misthollow. The pale towers are dark, and frost grows on the Moonwell’s stair.”

Io can feel the dead Moonwell from the gate: “It’s like a hole in the night.”

Ede at the Lantern House gives Sol her Hearth-Coal (+10 Heat at the start of every fight), and the inn is a free rest point. Sorrel sells herbs at band-4 prices, the dearest in the game. The frozen trough at the west end of the lower square holds the children’s letter to the Moon, and a Nightrose.

The balcony bridge: from the upper square’s west end by Sorrel’s, climb the west stairs north, then turn west along the balcony bridge to its end: the Misthollow Cowl, which halves Frost. You want it for Noctara. The stairs on up to the tower door are empty.

The dead Moonwell

{{map:moonwell}}

Get ready

Be level 20. Rest at the Lantern House. Carry at least one of every herb, above all a Nightrose and an Ember-star Lily. Wear the Misthollow Cowl and both of Sol’s Heat keepsakes.

Up the street

Take the long street north out of Misthollow to a vast round plaza and the dead Moonwell, a black shaft that gives no light. Step onto the head of the steps, between the braziers, and the finale begins with a cutscene (72 seconds, skippable; Settings → Watch again replays it).

“Noctara the Starless waits at the well, and Halcyon stands at her side.”

You fight both. If you fall, you wake at your last rest with all your herbs back and can try again as often as you like. Later tries skip the cutscene.

The ending

Win, and Io takes out the last of her letters. “Envoi’s last strike is the real sending.” The stars come back, Noctara becomes night with stars in it, and Halcyon goes home, a pale moth rising. The win pays 2,556 experience and 3,834 shards. Stay for the ending and the credits card, which counts Letters sent, Fights and play time.

Caution · Final boss

Noctara: 926,492 HP. Halcyon: 253,028 HP. Both level 20. Every action Noctara takes makes her hit 11.5% harder (“Cold +N%”). Kill Halcyon first, strike hard and early, and save Envoi and Lunara for Void Sphere. Plan in the Boss files.

“The cold deepens every turn: strike hard and early, and keep Envoi’s ward and Lunara for Void Sphere.”The game’s own hint, on the defeat card
After the credits

Choose Continue on the title and Io is back at the head of the dead Moonwell’s steps. There’s no golden arrow any more. For the first time you can walk the Moonwell’s court (towers, lanes and stairs, nothing to find). The Magpie, shops, rests and wild fights all still work, and any keepsakes or letters you missed are still out there.

New lines after the ending
  • Gretch: “The stars are back. I stood in the square half the night just looking at them.”
  • Sorrel: “My herbs have their color back. Isn’t that a silly thing to notice, after all that?”
  • Ede: “The Moonwell shines. I didn’t think I’d see it again.”
  • The watchwoman: “Nothing on the stair tonight but moonlight. I could get used to that.”
