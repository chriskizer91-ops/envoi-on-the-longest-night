Walkthrough · Part II · Band 2 · Levels 5–10

The Warm Roads

Foes level 6–10 · Keepsakes: 3 · Letters: 1 · Ember Line nodes: 3 · Gate fight: three wraiths. The Ember Line runs under the Warm Roads from node to node, all the way to Dawnroost. It should be warm underfoot. It isn’t.

P. 25

The Warm Roads camp

{{map:warm-roads-camp}}

From Bogmire’s west dock, fly north-west. The only new stop is The Warm Roads. The first landing plays a short scene (Sol: “It should be warm underfoot.” Io: “It’s cold.”), then offers a rest. The camp fire is a full rest point, and camps never have fights.

The camp’s side paths (north to a lookout rock, south over the stream, a nook by the west tent) are scenery: nothing on them, and the south path runs off the picture with no exit. The way on is east.

{{/map}}

The Ember Line road New map

{{map:ember-line-road}}

Random fights start again here: band-2 wisps and wraiths, level 6 to 10, fought in the Warm Roads’ moor arena.

Relight the Ember Line. Three dark nodes stand along this road, and the golden arrow ignores every one. Walk up to each: “A node, gone dark. Let me…” Sol lays her hand on the stone, her Heat runs into it, and it gives back 300 shards. That’s 900 shards for a short detour, and a big start on the 2,300 for the next upgrade.

  • Node 1: west of the bridge, take the side path north into the ring of standing stones.
  • Node 2: right beside the road, by the bridge.
  • Node 3: after the bridge, as the road bends north, take the side path east along the wall, then the narrow trail up the rise.

The way on is the road’s north exit, into the forest.

The forest road to Dawnroost New map

{{map:dawnroost-road}}

A straight walk north with random fights. The side trails (west to the pool, east to the woodcutter’s lean-to, the lantern’s verge) hold nothing yet. North leads to Dawnroost’s south gate.

{{/map}}

Dawnroost

{{map:dawnroost}}

Sol: “Dawnroost. It’s quieter than I remember. There were forty Wardens in that hall.” Io: “And now there’s you.”

Your first visit on foot opens Dawnroost’s landing. Talk to Marta at the Warden hall door: she gives Sol the Wardens’ Hall Gauntlets (“These hung over your bunk all the years you were gone.”), and the hall is a free rest point. Brann runs the forge and sells herbs at band-2 prices. Tamsin wants to know if you’re a real Warden.

The yard well in the middle holds Marta’s letter to the Wardens who never came home, and a Lavender. Sol: “She never stopped making them up. Every one.”

The wall-walk: just inside the north gate, a narrow way runs west between the hall roof and the north wall. It looks like scenery. Follow it to its west end for the Dawnroost Kettle-Helm, the watch’s helm with a little rooster weather vane on top.

Tip · Shopping trick

Herb prices depend on the shop’s band, not yours. Brann charges about 2.5× Nettie’s prices. The Magpie can always land at Wickhollow, so fly home to stock up when you’re rich.

Dawnroost’s living node

{{map:dawnroost-node}}

Through the north gate is a court paved with a cracked sun mosaic, and the last node on the Line still blazing. “And the wraiths know it.” Step in and three level-12 wraiths rise around it. Go in at level 10 or higher, 11 to be safe. There’s no Envoi yet: it’s made at the end of this fight. Plan in the Boss files.

Envoi is made. When the last moth rises, Io takes out the letters she was meant to burn and folds them into a wyrm, and Sol lights its heart from the node. From now on Io can summon Envoi once a battle while Sol has 70 Heat or more.

The Node Sunstone: climb the stair in the court’s north-west corner. A chip of the node’s sunstone has come loose at the top. The north-east stair has nothing, and neither does the hidden nook behind the weapon stall on the east side.

The charge. Back down in Dawnroost, talk to Brann. For 2,300 shards at level 10, the living node’s light pours into the Magpie’s keel, and band 3 opens: the northern wilds.

Caution · Gate fight

Each wraith has 19,318 HP and its own once-only Eclipse (about 2,700 to both heroes) below 35%. Three wraiths, three Eclipses. Kill them one at a time, and carry each from above 35% to zero in one burst.

Walkthrough · Part III · Band 3 · Levels 10–15

The north road

Foes level 11–15 · Keepsakes: 2 + a gift · Letters: 1 · Story fight: the Gloam Knight. “Halcyon came north eight years ago. My teacher. She never came back.” “Maybe we’ll find out why.”

P. 29

The northern camp

{{map:northern-camp}}

Fly north from Dawnroost’s Warden dock to The northern wilds. Sol tells you about Halcyon, then the camp offers a rest. The way on is east. Band 3 brings frost wisps (weak to fire) and, in one wild fight in five, a Bramble Horror: a blackberry thicket that wasn’t a thicket. Read the Bestiary before you wander.

{{/map}}

Eldergrove’s edge New map

{{map:eldergrove-edge}}

Random fights, band 3, fought in Eldergrove’s clearing arena. Side paths lead up into the hollow at the great tree’s foot, into a ring of standing stones (a moon stone and a sun stone among them), and under a ruined arch to an old path south that runs off the picture. None of them hold anything. East to the cold moor.

The northern crossroads

{{map:crossroads}}

Four old roads meet at a ruined plaza with a compass rose in its stones. Random fights happen here too. You arrive on the west road; the south and east roads turn you back.

The ambush

The first time you step into the stone ring, someone comes down the north road out of the dark: a knight in dark armor, blade already drawn. The Gloam Knight is level 20 whatever level you are.

Partway through, Sol recognizes the way this knight fights: “Halcyon?” A new command appears at the top of Sol’s menu: Kestrel. Use it once and Halcyon loses her next turn. It unlocks on her third turn, at half HP, or when she first takes her Vow, whichever comes first.

Two ways it ends

  • She retreats. Bring her down to 20% HP: “Halcyon steps back from them, her blade lowered.” Pays 2,556 experience and 3,834 shards, almost the whole moon-sail.
  • She spares you. If the whole party falls: Sol drags herself up and stands over Io, Halcyon’s blade stops, and she steps back into the dark. The end card reads “Spared.” You get nothing and the herbs you used stay used, but the story carries on exactly the same.

Either way, Sol learns Kestrel Stoop, the party rests by the well fully healed, and the road north opens. Sol: “She stopped. She looked at me, and she stopped.”

After the ambush

Now you can reach the crossroads well on the plaza’s south-west rim: Ennis’s letter (“To my love at the yard…”) and a Silver Mugwort. Then follow the east road almost to the map’s edge: a Wardens’ red pennant is tied to a waymark, the Crossroads Pennant. Sol: “They came this way. Some of them did.”

Secret · You can’t lose

This fight can’t go wrong. Win or lose, the story is the same and Sol learns Kestrel Stoop. You’re fighting for the payout. At level 15 you won’t drive her off; at 18 it’s about 3 in 5 for sharp play; at 19, 9 in 10.

Caution · Changed

The earlier guide’s trick of reading the well before the ambush no longer works. From the west road, every way to the well goes through the knight’s trigger.

The shipyard

{{map:shipyard}}

The north road leads over a long stone bridge to a snowy harbour fortress. Your first visit on foot opens its landing. Ysmera Brightkeel, the Aurosi shipmaster, meets you inside the gatehouse and asks about a knight with a black sun on her hilt. “That is the Starless’s mark. Noctara’s. The knight serves her.” Noctara will be at Misthollow’s Moonwell on the longest night.

Talk to Ysmera again for Ysmera’s Moonglass (+10% MP for Io). Pim sells herbs, Tock admires your skiff, and Old Gil’s bunkhouse is a free rest point.

The cove dock: from the yard by the bunkhouse go west onto the terrace and down the stairs to the lower quay. At the end of the west pier: the Dockhand’s Gloves, +10% shards from every fight.

The moon-sail. Ysmera’s gnomes will rig the Magpie with a moon-sail of Aurosi silk for 4,500 shards at level 15. That opens band 4: the Ironspire Peaks.

Tip · Short on shards?

Wear the Forge Horseshoe and the Dockhand’s Gloves for +15% shards from every win. And if you drove Halcyon off, her 3,834 shards nearly cover the moon-sail alone.
