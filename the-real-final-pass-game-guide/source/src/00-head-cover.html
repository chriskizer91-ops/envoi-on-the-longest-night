From the editors

Three nights before the longest night of the year, the stars over Wickhollow go out. Io, the village witch, keeps the Moonwell and a bundle of letters to the dead she has never been able to burn. By the last page of this guide she will have flown a patched-up skiff over the Ironspire Peaks, folded those letters into a wyrm, and stood at a dead Moonwell against Noctara the Starless.

An earlier guide to this game went around in early October. This is the one to keep. It covers the final polish build, the copy Chris has as one file, and it was written by playing that file and reading the game’s own rules. Every number in these pages is the game’s: what each spell costs, how hard it hits, how much a Nightrose costs in the far north, and where every keepsake is lying in the dark.

A lot moved between the two builds. The world map is now flown only, eight new roads link the Magpie’s camps to the towns, two old shortcuts are gone and one old trick no longer works. The What’s new pages list all of it.

How to read this guide

  • Tip boxes hold strategy. Secret boxes point at things the game never tells you. Caution boxes mean a fight that can go badly if you walk in unready. Item boxes mark keepsakes and gifts.
  • Numbered pins on the maps match the list beside each map. Tap a pin to light up its line.
  • Damage numbers are averages at the level you’ll likely be. Every hit rolls up to 25% either way.

Which build?

This guide covers “Envoi on the Longest Night - final polish.html”, built October 5, 2026, with the four try-page ideas switched on: footsteps, words that move on by themselves, the door sound between maps, and Buy 10 in herb shops.
